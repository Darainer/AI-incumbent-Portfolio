# Source register

Research cut-off: 2026-09-11. Links below were opened during this build. Publication dates may be older than the access date. Only the specific evidence stated is claimed as verified. Company marketing receives less weight than independently measured outcomes. No full third-party reports are redistributed.

## S01 — Generative AI at Work, revised November 2024

[Research paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.11771v2) · Published: 2024-11-06 · Accessed: 2026-09-11

**Supported:** 5,172 support agents. AI assistance increased issues resolved per hour by 15% on average in this setting.

**Limit:** Task-level evidence in one setting, not a forecast for all firms or proof of retained profit.

## S02 — METR experienced developer randomized trial

[Research organization](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/) · Published: 2025-07-10 · Accessed: 2026-09-11

**Supported:** 16 developers and 246 tasks took 19% longer with early-2025 AI tools.

**Limit:** Historical tools and selected experienced developers. Read S18 before using this as a current capability assessment.
