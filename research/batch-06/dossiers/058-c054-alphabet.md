## Provisional conclusion and conventional business

Alphabet has a credible incumbent-AI mechanism because it controls search intent, advertiser conversion signals, YouTube content/distribution, Android and proprietary TPUs. The scoped evidence assessment is **E2 external Cloud AI demand in opened Q2 results; own-productivity/capture unresolved**. It does **not** establish controlled quality-adjusted improvement across a representative workload or incremental cash retained by shareholders. The investment question therefore remains operational: can management disclose a stable baseline, eligible workload, quality guardrail, realized economics and recurring technology cost?

The latest conventional anchor is [Q2 2026 (ended 30 June 2026)](https://abc.xyz/investor/static/pdf/2026Q2_alphabet_earnings_release.pdf): **$119.8bn revenue, up 24%; Google Services $94.5bn and Cloud $24.8bn** and **$40.8bn operating income and 34% margin; Cloud operating income $8.8bn**. These figures are reported unless explicitly labelled adjusted. $39.1bn Q2 operating cash flow, negative $5.9bn Q2 free cash flow after $44.9bn capex. $242.5bn cash and marketable securities reported against $98.2bn long-term debt; equity-security valuation drove large other income. The principal confounders are cycle, mix, pricing, acquisitions/restructuring, working capital and capital intensity. Existing prediction, optimization and automation are already in these reported results; only incremental improvement from here belongs in an AI bridge.

## AI mechanisms and competitive challenge

**Mechanism 1 — scoped workflow improvement.** AI Overviews/Mode can improve query satisfaction and ad matching, but must preserve traffic, advertiser returns and publisher ecosystem health.

**Mechanism 2 — execution or adjacent demand.** Gemini/Vertex AI and TPU capacity monetize external infrastructure and model demand; capex and depreciation are central, and this is distinct from internal efficiency.

**Challenger and substitute.** Microsoft/OpenAI threatens search and productivity interfaces; AWS and Azure challenge cloud, while publishers/regulators constrain data and distribution.

**Adverse evidence.** Q2 capex was $44.9bn and 2026 guidance $195–205bn; antitrust remedies, content rights and query cannibalization can shift value away from shareholders.

## Measurement contract and materiality arithmetic

The framework’s research convention is **$8bn annual benefit, roughly 5% of a $160bn operating-profit run-rate**. This is a scale test, not a forecast or valuation.

| KPI / unit | Baseline and comparator | Eligible workload | Quality condition | Known result / next proof event |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Search contribution/query | pre-AI query cohort | AI Overview/Mode queries | same user satisfaction and publisher quality | randomized query monetization |
| Cloud gross profit/compute unit | prior GCP mix | AI capacity consumed | after TPU/server/DC depreciation and power | contract cohort and FCF bridge |

Illustrative cost bridge: `$60bn eligible internal support, engineering and operations cost (assumption) × 10% task efficiency × 50% realization × 50% retained share − $5bn recurring AI expense = negative $3.5bn annual operating benefit`. All bridge inputs are assumptions. The result is below the hurdle. It excludes customer savings; any revenue case must use incremental company contribution after cannibalization, servicing and model cost. Capex and working capital remain separate.

## Hypotheses, falsifiers and evidence gates

**Strongest positive hypothesis:** the installed asset and distribution let Alphabet embed models in a difficult workflow, observe outcomes and retain part of better throughput, decisions or service. **Strongest negative hypothesis:** generic models and rivals reach the same customer context, contractual rights block pooled learning, and customers, workers or suppliers capture the gain while implementation/capital cost remains with the company.

Three falsifiers: (1) by the next two annual reporting cycles, management still cannot disclose active production penetration and a quality-adjusted KPI for a material workload; (2) renewals, usage or contribution margin deteriorate as AI pricing, cannibalization and supplier costs rise; (3) matched rollouts fail to beat the existing production system after errors, rework, safety/risk and implementation cost. Core-business deterioration must be diagnosed separately from AI failure.

Unresolved questions are the precise customer-data licenses; whether feedback may improve a pooled model; eligible workload; representative baseline; error and escalation rate; incremental price/volume; labor realization; recurring inference/support cost; required capex; and the share of savings retained after competition or regulation.

**G0:** provisionally satisfied: asset, mechanisms, capture hypothesis and falsifiers are explicit. **G1:** provisionally satisfied only subject to the stated balance-sheet, governance and rights gaps. **G2:** provisional **watch / incomplete**: E2 external Cloud AI demand in opened Q2 results; own-productivity/capture unresolved; E3 quality-adjusted evidence and E4 retained cash are absent. **G3/G4:** deferred by user. Confidence is **moderate** in mechanism, **low-to-moderate** in broad deployment and **low** in capture.
