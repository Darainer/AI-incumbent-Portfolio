## Provisional conclusion

KLA has a durable conventional franchise based on inspection images, metrology physics, installed tools, defect libraries and application engineers. The public evidence supports specific AI capabilities and, in some cases, management-reported operational use. It does not provide a controlled, quality-adjusted company result tied to retained incremental cash. The central underwriting issue is capture: operational improvement may pass to customers, be absorbed by implementation, already sit in the reported baseline, or be dominated by cycle and mix. The dossier therefore keeps G2 provisional and treats E3/E4 as unestablished.

## Conventional business and financial anchor

KLA reports Semiconductor Process Control, Specialty Semiconductor Process and PCB/Component Inspection. Customer value arises from dependable execution, integration and domain expertise, not data volume alone. Competitive advantage must survive Onto Innovation and fab-built analytics and inspection modules bundled by broader equipment vendors. Switching costs and distribution help, but contracts, interoperability and procurement can reallocate gains.

For FY2026 ended 30 June 2026, issuer reporting identifies **$13.58bn revenue** and **$4.83bn GAAP net income**. This after-tax net-income metric governs the hurdle throughout the packet; earlier FY2025 operating-income figures are excluded.

The [FY2026 issuer release](https://ir.kla.com/) reports $13.58bn annual revenue and $4.83bn GAAP net income. KLA product literature describes ML defect classification and process control, but this packet found no controlled fab-level false-positive/yield result and no customer contract proving pooled reuse. The production-tool context supports E1 and possible management-reported E2 only where a named installed product is documented.

The ordinary baseline already contains deployed analytics, automation and machine learning. Capital allocation must fund maintenance, growth investment, obligations and shareholder distributions before any incremental AI benefit. Financial scale permits experimentation, but it does not demonstrate quality, adoption or capture.

## Measurement contract and retained economics

Pre-register **actionable defects found per wafer-hour at fixed false-positive rate, yield learning-cycle time, tool availability and service margin**. Compare randomized or carefully matched sites, clients, products or cohorts against the current production system, including existing ML. Define the eligible workload before rollout; hold service, safety, accuracy, risk and customer-mix quality constant. Report sample size, duration, confidence interval, adoption, exception/rework rate and all human/model/implementation cost. The known public result is insufficient for E3. A future annual report, product cohort study or customer renewal disclosure linking the KPI to contribution could close the gap.

The framework's annual research convention is **$241.5m**: 5% × FY2026 GAAP net income of $4.83bn, after tax. The illustrative pre-tax bridge is $1.5bn eligible pool ×12%×60%×50%−$35m = $19m; applying an explicit 20% research tax assumption gives **$15.2m after tax**, below the hurdle. Customer fab savings are not KLA cost savings.

## Competing hypotheses, falsifiers and gates

**Strongest positive hypothesis:** proprietary workflow context and distribution let KLA deploy validated AI faster than standalone vendors, with better feedback and a retained service, pricing, throughput or capital benefit. **Strongest negative hypothesis:** model capability commoditizes, customer rights prevent pooling, implementation and verification absorb gains, and Onto Innovation or fab-built analytics and inspection modules bundled by broader equipment vendors forces pass-through.

Three falsifiers are: (1) matched deployments fail to improve actionable defects found per wafer-hour at fixed false-positive rate, yield learning-cycle time, tool availability and service margin at equal quality; (2) five-year retained benefit after recurring AI and implementation cost remains below the stated 5% hurdle; (3) contracts, portability or competitor performance show that the alleged data/workflow advantage is readily replicated. Unresolved questions are eligible-workload share, exact production penetration, customer-level retention/pricing, recurring model cost, contract training rights and the latest post-FY2026 adverse development.

G0 passes because asset, mechanism, capture route and falsifiers are explicit. G1 is provisionally supported by the conventional franchise and financial capacity, subject to latest-period refresh and sector risks. G2 remains **open**: scoped E1/E2 can support further testing, but E3/E4 are absent. G3 and G4 are **deferred by user**. Confidence is **medium** for mechanism, **low-to-medium** for deployment breadth and **low** for capture.
