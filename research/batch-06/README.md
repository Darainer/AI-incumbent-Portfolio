## Lead acceptance notes

**Dassault Systèmes (C046):** Design and virtual-twin capabilities require real-world validation and paid adoption. Product availability stays E1; the quarterly operating screen does not establish annual materiality or retained design savings.

**Autodesk (C047):** Workflow access gives a plausible route to paid design/construction assistance, but availability is not measured use. Rework, correctness, attach and renewal must be observed together. The H1 screen is period-specific.

**TSMC (C048):** AI infrastructure demand is separate from TSMC internal fab productivity. Internal enterprise-AI programme language remains E1; no matched yield, downtime or own-cost outcome was established.

**ASML (C049):** Computational lithography provides a product mechanism, with major hardware and physics confounders. Current net-income and after-tax illustrations are aligned, but incremental software pricing and customer benefit capture remain assumptions.

**Applied Materials (C050):** AIx combines process tools and analytics, but production expectations remain E1. Latest Q3 context is included. Customer yield improvement is not Applied’s own expense saving or proven software contribution.

**KLA (C051):** The governing screen uses current full-year GAAP net income and a tax-aligned illustration. Defect classification must control nuisance rates and inspection quality; product capability is not isolated retained cash.

**Microsoft (C052):** Paid Copilot seats and Azure AI demand establish external adoption. The own-productivity illustration uses an assumed expense pool only. Capex, depreciation and reinvestment prevent a direct usage-to-profit conclusion.

**Alphabet (C054):** Search and Cloud AI are deployed, but growth and aggregate profit do not isolate AI effects. Traffic mix, TAC, inference and infrastructure spending require a net contribution bridge; the quarterly screen is not annual materiality.

**Accenture (C055):** The filed Q3 result is corroborated. AI services demand/bookings are commercial evidence, not recurring profit. Billable-hour cannibalization, client sharing and reskilling costs matter; the quarter screen is not an annual comparison.

**Tata Consultancy Services (C056):** The current Q1 release supports the AI revenue run rate and named deals. Run rate is not audited annual revenue, and adjusted operating income excludes an exceptional item. Retained contribution and the annual-period bridge remain open.

Acceptance checks source support, scope, materiality arithmetic and explicit limits. It is neither an audit opinion nor a buy/sell recommendation. Raw capsules preserve the researcher's handoff; lead notes govern unresolved differences.
