## Lead acceptance notes

**Moody's (C030):** Permissioned credit content is a plausible workflow advantage, but customer production is not independently established. The exact H1 operating anchor replaces an undefined annual estimate; its period screen cannot be compared with annual benefits.

**London Stock Exchange Group (C032):** Named customer use supports scoped E2. Microsoft distribution does not settle upstream licensing, interface dependence or seat cannibalization. The H1 operating screen is not an annual materiality test.

**Intercontinental Exchange (C033):** Mortgage-system integration offers a plausible capture route through workflow fees. Product checks and AI positioning do not establish lower defect-adjusted processing cost or paid upsell; the assumed revenue bridge remains unproved.

**Veeva Systems (C038):** Vault configuration and agent actions establish available capability. Treat product availability as weaker than demonstrated customer production. Validated outputs, paid upsell and inference/support expense are the missing bridge.

**Constellation Software (C039):** No consolidated named AI production result was found across the decentralized portfolio. Subsidiary/customer rights cannot be pooled by assumption. The corrected attributable-income anchor is after tax; vertical copilots remain a hypothesis.

**ADP (C040):** Payroll workflow distribution supports a mechanism, not demonstrated AI economics. Corrected current-year net earnings governs the screen; expert escalation, error rates and customer-specific rights remain central.

**ServiceNow (C042):** Issuer AI contract value and deployment growth support commercial adoption, not autonomous resolution quality or contribution margin. Distinguish ACV from revenue; the annual guidance screen is an explicitly approximate non-GAAP proxy.

**Salesforce (C043):** Reported Agentforce commercial use is scoped to its disclosed, changing ARR definition. Work units do not establish correctness or resolved demand. Seat cannibalization, inference cost and rights remain open.

**Synopsys (C044):** Issuer financials and AI design products support capability. Broad vendor speed/PPA claims lack a matched project portfolio and signoff-quality economics; treat E2 as conditional. Quarter/9M screens cannot be compared with annual assumptions.

**Cadence Design Systems (C045):** Current GAAP margin and revenue are corroborated. AI design capability is clear, but controlled signoff quality and net vendor contribution are absent. The corrected illustration is negative USD14m; annualization remains a scale assumption.

Acceptance checks source support, scope, materiality arithmetic and explicit limits. It is neither an audit opinion nor a buy/sell recommendation. Raw capsules preserve the researcher's handoff; lead notes govern unresolved differences.
