**Identity/boundary.** Veeva Systems Inc. Class A, NYSE:VEEV, USD. Cutoff/retrieval 12 September 2026; latest fiscal Q2 2027 ended 31 July, released 26 August. Unreviewed; G3/G4 deferred. Vault AI agents are available in commercial workflows and can execute configured actions over governed application context. Evidence remains vendor-reported availability/adoption; no controlled end-to-end productivity, error or net contribution cohort is public.

## Business and financial resilience

Veeva supplies life-science cloud software/data/services across commercial CRM/content and R&D clinical, regulatory, safety and quality workflows. Subscriptions produce recurring revenue; validated configurations, audit trails, data models and integrations raise switching costs. Migration from Salesforce-based CRM to Vault CRM creates execution risk and opportunity. Salesforce, IQVIA and specialized clinical/regulatory vendors compete; customers can build agents above their data layer.

FY2026 revenue was **$3.195bn**, subscription revenue **$2.684bn**, GAAP operating income **$916.4m**, and Q4 GAAP operating income $245.9m. [FY2026 results](https://ir.veeva.com/news/news-details/2026/Veeva-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2026-Results/default.aspx), 4 March 2026. Fiscal Q2 2027 revenue was **$928.0m**, up 18%; GAAP operating income **$275.0m**, up 40%; non-GAAP operating income **$416m**, 45% margin. [Q2 results](https://ir.veeva.com/news/news-details/2026/Veeva-Announces-Fiscal-2027-Second-Quarter-Results/default.aspx), 26 August 2026. Stock compensation drives a large GAAP/non-GAAP gap. Growth also reflects Vault CRM migrations and ordinary cross-sell, so cannot be attributed to AI. Veeva historically carries substantial cash/investments and no material conventional debt, but exact Q2 balance must be taken from the 10-Q before valuation.

## Mechanisms/challengers

1. **Commercial agents (agentic/generative).** CRM call notes, approved content and activity → pre-call suggestions, voice capture and free-text compliance flags → less admin and better customer engagement → AI subscription/retention. Agents became available December 2025. This is E2 product availability; no eligible seat/task denominator or controlled quality result.
2. **Regulatory/safety/content agents.** Vault documents/objects → intake, completeness checks, authoring and triage → faster cycle time at equal audit/accuracy → upsell and lower support burden. Roadmap/demo evidence is E1; availability varies by app and customer enablement.
3. **Customer-built agents.** Admins configure agent actions in Vault AI Chat. This reinforces platform distribution but opens substitution: customers can connect external models through APIs. Human approval, validation and model cost limit autonomy.

Salesforce Life Sciences Cloud is the incumbent challenger; IQVIA and Microsoft/custom agents are substitutes. Per-seat cannibalization is real if agents let fewer users perform the same work. Customers retain process savings and may resist pricing; base-model suppliers take inference economics.

Five percent of FY2026 GAAP OP is **$45.8m/year**. Illustrative recurring bridge: $2.684bn subscription base × 8% AI upsell × 35% adoption × 80% gross margin × 70% retention after seat cannibalization = **$42.1m/year**; less $15m recurring inference/validation/support gives **$27.1m**. Internal support savings: $500m eligible cost ×10% efficiency×40% realization×60% retention = $12m, total **$39.1m**, below hurdle. Required upsell holding adoption is `(45.8+15-12)/(2684×.35×.8×.7)=9.3%`. All inputs except reported financials are assumptions.

## Hypotheses/falsifiers/gates

Positive: governed life-science workflows let agents execute valuable regulated tasks and Veeva prices the resulting platform expansion. Negative: customer data stays siloed, generic agents improve, validation blunts productivity and seat contraction offsets upsell.

Falsifiers: AI cohorts show no net ACV after seat/inference effects; error/audit rates require near-total human duplication; customers favor model-neutral API layers over Veeva agents. Questions: active tenants/users, task success and edits, pricing, base models, training rights and net margin.

Confidence mechanism high, deployment medium, capture low-medium. G0/G1 provisional pass; G2 provisional/insufficient: E2 product availability but no controlled result or retained cash. G3/G4 deferred.
