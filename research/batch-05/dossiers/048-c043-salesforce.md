## Provisional conclusion and conventional business

Salesforce has a credible incumbent-AI mechanism because it controls CRM system-of-record context, customer-configured permissions, metadata and distribution. The scoped evidence assessment is **E1 agentic CRM product capability; ARR/work-unit release was not successfully opened**. It does **not** establish controlled quality-adjusted improvement across a representative workload or incremental cash retained by shareholders. The investment question therefore remains operational: can management disclose a stable baseline, eligible workload, quality guardrail, realized economics and recurring technology cost?

The latest conventional anchor is [Q2 FY2027 (ended 31 July 2026)](https://investor.salesforce.com/financials/default.aspx): **FY2027 revenue guidance raised to $46.1–46.4bn, 11–12% growth** and **management reported more than $1bn Agentforce ARR and $3.4bn combined AI and data ARR in Q1; GAAP/non-GAAP margins differ materially**. These figures are reported unless explicitly labelled adjusted. operating cash flow benefits from annual billings; capex is modest but acquisitions and stock compensation are economic costs. cash, debt and acquired intangibles must be reconciled; Informatica integration adds execution risk. The principal confounders are cycle, mix, pricing, acquisitions/restructuring, working capital and capital intensity. Existing prediction, optimization and automation are already in these reported results; only incremental improvement from here belongs in an AI bridge.

## AI mechanisms and competitive challenge

**Mechanism 1 — scoped workflow improvement.** Agentforce can qualify leads or resolve service cases through workflow tools; production work units and ARR establish E2 breadth but not quality-adjusted resolution.

**Mechanism 2 — execution or adjacent demand.** Data Cloud harmonizes customer-specific context; cross-customer training rights remain separate and undisclosed by default.

**Challenger and substitute.** Microsoft Dynamics/Copilot and ServiceNow compete at workflow interfaces; HubSpot and model-native agents can pressure seats.

**Adverse evidence.** agent execution can cannibalize user seats or shift value to foundation-model suppliers; work-unit counts omit correctness and customer economics.

## Measurement contract and materiality arithmetic

The framework’s research convention is **$800m, around 5% of a normalized $16bn non-GAAP operating-profit scale proxy**. This is a scale test, not a forecast or valuation.

| KPI / unit | Baseline and comparator | Eligible workload | Quality condition | Known result / next proof event |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Correct agent resolutions/1,000 | existing Service Cloud | Agentforce work units | same reopen, escalation and CSAT | customer renewal cohort |
| Net ARR/account | matched CRM accounts | AI/Data Cloud attach cohort | after seat cannibalization and model cost | ARR/NRR cohort reconciliation |

Illustrative cost bridge: `$10bn eligible internal sales, service and development operating cost (assumption) × 10% task efficiency × 50% realization × 40% retained share − $400m recurring AI expense = negative $200m annual operating benefit`. All bridge inputs are assumptions. The result is below the hurdle. It excludes customer savings; any revenue case must use incremental company contribution after cannibalization, servicing and model cost. Capex and working capital remain separate.

## Hypotheses, falsifiers and evidence gates

**Strongest positive hypothesis:** the installed asset and distribution let Salesforce embed models in a difficult workflow, observe outcomes and retain part of better throughput, decisions or service. **Strongest negative hypothesis:** generic models and rivals reach the same customer context, contractual rights block pooled learning, and customers, workers or suppliers capture the gain while implementation/capital cost remains with the company.

Three falsifiers: (1) by the next two annual reporting cycles, management still cannot disclose active production penetration and a quality-adjusted KPI for a material workload; (2) renewals, usage or contribution margin deteriorate as AI pricing, cannibalization and supplier costs rise; (3) matched rollouts fail to beat the existing production system after errors, rework, safety/risk and implementation cost. Core-business deterioration must be diagnosed separately from AI failure.

Unresolved questions are the precise customer-data licenses; whether feedback may improve a pooled model; eligible workload; representative baseline; error and escalation rate; incremental price/volume; labor realization; recurring inference/support cost; required capex; and the share of savings retained after competition or regulation.

**G0:** provisionally satisfied: asset, mechanisms, capture hypothesis and falsifiers are explicit. **G1:** provisionally satisfied only subject to the stated balance-sheet, governance and rights gaps. **G2:** provisional **watch / incomplete**: E1 agentic CRM product capability; ARR/work-unit release was not successfully opened; E3 quality-adjusted evidence and E4 retained cash are absent. **G3/G4:** deferred by user. Confidence is **moderate** in mechanism, **low-to-moderate** in broad deployment and **low** in capture.
