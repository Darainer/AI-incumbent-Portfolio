# First 18 diligence questions

This queue tests whether productivity optionality is underpriced. It does not assert that any of these companies is currently cheap. P1 selection balances content/workflow, risk decisions, physical operations and scientific discovery. Sequence within the queue is a research order, not a portfolio allocation.
