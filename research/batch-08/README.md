## Lead acceptance notes

**Waste Management (C070):** Recycling automation is deployed, but its AI-specific contribution is not isolated. Routing, maintenance and sorting claims require separate proof; the quarterly profit screen is not comparable with annual cost assumptions.

**Cintas (C071):** Cintas has route density and operating records, but no named AI deployment was established. The corrected annual operating-profit anchor is usable for scale; route/plant savings remain hypotheses subject to service and retention controls.

**BASF (C073):** Predictive maintenance in the retrieved BASF material establishes a digital baseline, not an identified AI technique. The AI thesis remains a hypothesis; site reliability, production economics and disposal effects must be separated.

**dsm-firmenich (C074):** Named formulation and sensory tools support company-reported use. Faster candidate generation needs prospective win rate, quality and retained contribution. Continuing operations and the latest H1 update are separated from disposal effects.

**Rio Tinto (C075):** Reported banked productivity is not attributed to AI. Commodity, mine and capital effects dominate aggregate results; the after-tax illustration is aligned but autonomous/processing contribution remains unisolated.

**BHP (C076):** Autonomous haulage and decision support are management-reported operating uses. Current annual profit is after tax. No isolated AI increment to safety-adjusted output, capital efficiency or owner cash was established.

**Alibaba (C077):** The opened SEC release establishes external Cloud/AI demand and the current profit anchor. Keep segment growth separate from AI-only product growth and commerce productivity. Heavy investment and new equity funding affect owner capture.

**MercadoLibre (C078):** The issuer reports production AI search improvements and related third-party model cost, but gives no controlled uplift magnitude. Developer testimonials are scoped E2. Neither supports group E4 cash attribution.

**Inditex (C079):** The researcher extracted current H1 results; exact financial pages were intermittently inaccessible to the lead. No named AI deployment was established. Forecasting/design mechanisms and annual retained economics remain hypotheses.

**Uber (C080):** Michelangelo provides direct evidence of extensive baseline ML deployment. New generative functionality needs incremental tests. Net income includes investment gains; the quarterly operating screen is not comparable with the annual illustration.

Acceptance checks source support, scope, materiality arithmetic and explicit limits. It is neither an audit opinion nor a buy/sell recommendation. Raw capsules preserve the researcher's handoff; lead notes govern unresolved differences.
