## Provisional conclusion and conventional business

MercadoLibre has a credible incumbent-AI mechanism because it controls Latin American buyer/seller graph, payments and repayment outcomes, logistics network and local execution. The scoped evidence assessment is **E2 named enterprise developer deployment in opened supplier/customer material; no E3/E4**. It does **not** establish controlled quality-adjusted improvement across a representative workload or incremental cash retained by shareholders. The investment question therefore remains operational: can management disclose a stable baseline, eligible workload, quality guardrail, realized economics and recurring technology cost?

The latest conventional anchor is [Q2 2026 (ended 30 June 2026)](https://investor.mercadolibre.com/open-file?file=aHR0cHM6Ly9odHRwMi5tbHN0YXRpYy5jb20vc3RvcmFnZS9tbC1jbXMtYmFja2VuZC9jbXMtZG9jdW1lbnRzLXByb2QvNWRiYmE5MTktNzIxYy00OTE2LWJiOTEtYzc2YTg3MTNmN2YwL2JkNjBhMzQyLWM1MjMtNDFiOC1hZjdhLWRhMjgxOGZlZjVmZi9NRUxJX1EyXzIwMjZfUHJlc3NfUmVsZWFzZS5wZGY): **$10.169bn net revenue and financial income, up 50%** and **$683m operating income (6.7% margin); $466m net income**. These figures are reported unless explicitly labelled adjusted. credit growth, receivables funding and logistics investment mean accounting profit is not simple platform FCF. fintech funding, credit-loss reserves, leases and logistics capex must be evaluated alongside corporate debt. The principal confounders are cycle, mix, pricing, acquisitions/restructuring, working capital and capital intensity. Existing prediction, optimization and automation are already in these reported results; only incremental improvement from here belongs in an AI bridge.

## AI mechanisms and competitive challenge

**Mechanism 1 — scoped workflow improvement.** MercadoLibre deployed GitHub Copilot across its developer organization. GitHub’s customer-story index says coding time fell 50%; MELI’s investor page says code submissions rose 90% year on year. These are identified production claims (E2), but neither source supplies matched quality, incidents or cash conversion. Credit/fraud models remain an established baseline.

**Mechanism 2 — execution or adjacent demand.** Fury and Spanner run transactional, recommendation and real-time risk workloads; Google Cloud’s 3 November 2025 account identifies 214,000 queries and 30,000 transactions per second plus operationalized fraud scores. This is production infrastructure evidence, not causal proof of incremental AI profit.

**Challenger and substitute.** Amazon, Shopee and local retailers contest commerce; Nubank and banks contest payments/credit, while logistics providers can unbundle fulfillment.

**Adverse evidence.** Q2 operating margin was only 6.7% despite 50% revenue growth; credit mix and heavy investment can obscure whether AI adds retained cash.

## Measurement contract and materiality arithmetic

The framework’s research convention is **$136.6m: 5% × (4 × Q2 reported operating income of $683m)**. This is a scale test, not a forecast or valuation.

| KPI / unit | Baseline and comparator | Eligible workload | Quality condition | Known result / next proof event |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accepted code cycle time | pre-Copilot teams | enabled developers/repos | same incidents, rework and security | GitHub reports 50% coding-time cut; verify matched design |
| Credit loss bps at fixed approval | incumbent score | AI-scored originations | same geography/tenor and collections | vintage holdout plus funding cost |

Illustrative cost bridge: `$3bn eligible logistics, fraud, service and merchant-support pool × 10% task efficiency × 40% realization × 50% retained share − $100m recurring AI expense = negative $40m annual operating benefit`. All bridge inputs are assumptions. The result is below the hurdle. It excludes customer savings; any revenue case must use incremental company contribution after cannibalization, servicing and model cost. Capex and working capital remain separate.

## Hypotheses, falsifiers and evidence gates

**Strongest positive hypothesis:** the installed asset and distribution let MercadoLibre embed models in a difficult workflow, observe outcomes and retain part of better throughput, decisions or service. **Strongest negative hypothesis:** generic models and rivals reach the same customer context, contractual rights block pooled learning, and customers, workers or suppliers capture the gain while implementation/capital cost remains with the company.

Three falsifiers: (1) by the next two annual reporting cycles, management still cannot disclose active production penetration and a quality-adjusted KPI for a material workload; (2) renewals, usage or contribution margin deteriorate as AI pricing, cannibalization and supplier costs rise; (3) matched rollouts fail to beat the existing production system after errors, rework, safety/risk and implementation cost. Core-business deterioration must be diagnosed separately from AI failure.

Unresolved questions are the precise customer-data licenses; whether feedback may improve a pooled model; eligible workload; representative baseline; error and escalation rate; incremental price/volume; labor realization; recurring inference/support cost; required capex; and the share of savings retained after competition or regulation.

**G0:** provisionally satisfied: asset, mechanisms, capture hypothesis and falsifiers are explicit. **G1:** provisionally satisfied only subject to the stated balance-sheet, governance and rights gaps. **G2:** provisional **watch / incomplete**: E2 named enterprise developer deployment in opened supplier/customer material; no E3/E4; E3 quality-adjusted evidence and E4 retained cash are absent. **G3/G4:** deferred by user. Confidence is **moderate** in mechanism, **low-to-moderate** in broad deployment and **low** in capture.
