# BASF — evidence packet

**Identity.** BASF SE ordinary shares Xetra:BAS, EUR. Cutoff 12 September 2026; latest H1 2026 released 29 July. Unreviewed; G3/G4 deferred. BASF uses predictive maintenance and digital process tools, but company-specific controlled energy/uptime/formulation economics are not disclosed; restructuring and asset sales dominate financial changes.

## Business/financials

BASF operates integrated chemical Verbund sites and segments in Chemicals, Materials, Industrial Solutions, Nutrition & Care, Surface Technologies and Agricultural Solutions. Integrated sites, process know-how, formulations, customer qualification and physical assets are scarce. Cyclicality, European energy, China capacity, commodity pricing and environmental liabilities are risks; Dow, Evonik and Chinese producers compete.

H1 2026 sales **€33.226bn**, reported EBIT **€2.198bn**, EBIT before special items **€2.913bn**, OCF **-€273m**, FCF **-€1.564bn**, net debt **€17.117bn**, equity ratio 44.6%. [BASF key data](https://www.basf.com/global/en/investors/basf-at-a-glance/key-financial-data), 29 July 2026. Coatings disposal produced a €3.9bn gain and discontinued operations, making €5.072bn net income non-comparable. 2026 EBITDA-before-special guidance €6.9–7.7bn and FCF €1.5–2.3bn reflect continuing restructuring. AI must be separated from cost cuts, pricing and portfolio effects.

**Predictive maintenance digital baseline:** sensor/history and special analysis software can support failure analysis and lower unplanned downtime at equal safety/output. The cited BASF page does not call this AI or machine learning, so it establishes a digital operating baseline only, not qualifying AI deployment. **Process optimization:** operating conditions → setpoint/energy suggestions → lower energy/tonne; physical control and safety constrain autonomy. **Formulation/R&D:** experiment/customer requirements → candidates → fewer cycles, but qualification is the bottleneck and no prospective result is public.

Dow is incumbent challenger; equipment/cloud analytics and startups substitute. Customers capture lower price/emissions; suppliers capture software fees.

### Attribution and operating constraints

A valid attribution design must separate model recommendations from the installed control system and from ordinary reliability engineering. For maintenance, BASF should pre-register eligible asset classes and compare a stepped rollout across matched trains, counting avoided failure hours only when a counterfactual work order or failure model supports them. A fall in downtime after deployment can otherwise reflect turnarounds, spare-part availability, demand-driven lower utilization or recently replaced equipment. Safety overrides and operator acceptance should be reported because an alert that is ignored cannot produce cash savings.

Energy optimization needs an equally strict boundary. The numerator should be metered gas, electricity or steam; the denominator should be specification-conforming tonnes. Weather, product mix, capacity utilization and energy prices should be controls. Price savings alone are not a model outcome, while physical energy reduction is. If recommendations merely shift output between plants, group benefit must include logistics and working-capital effects. BASF owns much of its plant telemetry, but equipment suppliers can retain diagnostic access and cloud providers can charge recurring fees. The scarce part is the combination of long-run histories with chemistry and operating context, not raw sensor volume.

Formulation is economically different from plant optimization. Candidate ranking can reduce low-value experiments, yet customer qualification and regulatory testing determine when revenue begins. Public disclosure does not show a prospective cohort, qualified launches per R&D euro, or incremental contribution margin. A retrospective comparison would be vulnerable to teams assigning easier briefs to the new tool. For this reason, the dossier does not convert hypothetical faster discovery into current annual profit.

### Capture and competitive response

BASF can capture benefits through lower conversion cost, higher asset availability, premium service or faster specialty-product launches. Capture will vary by segment. Commodity customers can demand price reductions and erase some producer savings; specialty applications may permit higher retention when the formula solves a costly customer problem. Software, sensor and equipment vendors can also absorb savings through subscriptions and integration work. Dow's own digital-manufacturing program is evidence that the broad capability is available to large chemical incumbents, so defensibility depends on BASF-specific data quality, workflow integration and scale of rollout.

The financial baseline is unusually noisy. Asset disposals, restructuring, utilization and European energy exposure can move EBIT by amounts larger than the illustrative AI bridge. Management cost initiatives must therefore be treated as the baseline unless a disclosed project ledger isolates AI-enabled changes. Cash conversion matters as well: H1 free cash flow was negative while net debt was €17.117bn. A claimed accounting benefit should be reconciled to maintenance spending, inventory, severance, cloud expense and avoided capital expenditure before being called retained cash.

## KPI/materiality

KPI contract: downtime hours/equipment-year versus matched prior units, equal safety/output; kWh or steam/tonne versus existing controls, equal specification; qualified formulations per R&D euro versus prior cohorts, equal customer acceptance. Known result: undisclosed. Close with site-level stepped rollout and audited cost bridge.

Five percent of H1 reported EBIT annualized is **€219.8m/year** (`2.198×2×5%`). Illustration: €15bn eligible annual production cost×3% efficiency×35% realization×60% retention−€50m recurring AI expense=**€44.5m/year**; €5bn specialty revenue×1% incremental contribution×35% margin×70% retention=€12.3m, total **€56.8m**. Required cost efficiency holding revenue path: `(219.8+50-12.3)/(15000×.35×.6)=8.2%`. Assumptions; ongoing cost cuts are baseline.

Positive: Verbund sensor data/formulation knowledge can improve capital productivity. Negative: cycle/energy/portfolio swamp effects, rights are fragmented and physical validation limits speed. Falsifiers: no matched energy/downtime improvement; qualified-product cycle unchanged; recurring costs exceed gains. Questions: eligible plants, realized cost, model vendors, formulation hit rates, disposal-adjusted baseline.

Disclosure that would change the conclusion is concrete: number and share of eligible assets under inference; false-positive and missed-failure rates; matched downtime and energy intensity; realized rather than modeled maintenance savings; active formulation users and qualified launches; and a recurring cost bridge including data engineering, compute and controls. Until those figures appear, deployment is plausible and operationally relevant but group materiality remains unverified.

Confidence mechanism medium-high, deployment medium-low, capture low-medium. G0 pass; G1 provisional given debt/cycle; G2 insufficient (no qualifying company-specific AI deployment evidence in the opened sources); G3/G4 deferred.
