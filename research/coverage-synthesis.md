Capability, production use, controlled improvement and retained cash remain separate claims.

## Findings that govern the next phase

**Evidence belongs to a mechanism and population.** Medtronic's GI Genius study supplies a narrowly scoped clinical result: adenoma detection was 59.1% versus 46.6% in 795 patients. Its quasi-randomized, non-blinded design and subgroup limits matter. A non-significant difference in non-neoplastic resection does not establish equivalence, and the study does not demonstrate mortality benefit or Medtronic cash capture. The packet retains scoped E3 evidence with these restrictions. [Original study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40018072/), [Medtronic dossier](batch-03/dossiers/023-c008-medtronic.md).

Meta's advertised 22% return-on-ad-spend uplift remains E2 company-reported evidence. The accessible announcement refers to randomized research, but the linked study could not be inspected sufficiently to verify that the specific AI-versus-baseline contrast was randomized. Advertiser revenue also differs from Meta's revenue or profit. That distinction prevented an unsupported E3 upgrade. [Meta announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/05/over-half-trillion-dollars-3-million-us-jobs-linked-metas-ai-driven-ads-technologies/), [Meta dossier](batch-10/dossiers/094-c094-meta-platforms.md).

**Deployment metrics require an economic bridge.** User counts, model counts, time saved and available assistants can establish operational presence without proving incremental earnings. JPMorgan's stated intention to reinvest released capacity in growth makes a simple headcount-removal model inappropriate. P&G's supplier-reported model-deployment acceleration concerns the time needed to deploy models; it is not a measured percentage improvement in factory productivity. [JPMorgan COO letter](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/ir/annual-report/2025/ar-ceo-letter-jennifer-piepszak), [P&G supplier case study](https://www.microsoft.com/en/customers/story/25077-procter-and-gamble-iot-operations).

**The existing business must include existing ML.** Recommendation systems, fraud models, predictive maintenance and optimization already in production belong in the conventional baseline. Their entire historical value cannot be relabeled incremental generative-AI upside. Selling cloud capacity, chips or AI services is a different mechanism from improving the seller's own productivity. The Amazon, Microsoft, Alphabet, Alibaba, TSMC and services packets preserve that separation. Financial growth alone does not identify either mechanism's incremental contribution.

**A scarce asset needs usable rights and a capture mechanism.** Customer-specific context, proprietary content and pooled cross-customer learning are distinct advantages. Operational access does not establish permission to train, retain or pool information. Likewise, savings for farmers, advertisers, hospitals or software users do not automatically accrue to the supplier. The dossiers identify those ownership and bargaining questions rather than assigning the whole productivity surplus to shareholders.

**Materiality illustrations are scale tests, not base cases.** The corrections are economically meaningful: Adyen's stated assumptions produce a negative €19.28 million illustration, and Cadence's produce negative $14 million. Neither is a forecast. Annual benefits cannot be compared directly with quarterly or half-year earnings, pre-tax gains with after-tax income, programme present value with annual operating profit, or aggregate dollars with per-share FFO. The 21 NOT_COMPARABLE records require normalization before they can support comparable materiality judgments. [Adyen packet](batch-04/dossiers/039-c028-adyen.md), [Cadence packet](batch-05/dossiers/050-c045-cadence-design-systems.md), [arithmetic audit](source-audits/arithmetic.json).

## Boundaries for subsequent judgment

The [framework's G2 gate](../framework/research-standard.md) requires at least E2 and a credible route to controlled evidence and material net impact. It does not require E4 cash proof already to exist. Consequently, lack of disclosed retained cash is an unresolved underwriting question, not an automatic veto. Conversely, an accessible product page or a management productivity target does not satisfy G2 by itself. Agent gate suggestions remain provisional; the lead notes govern evidence qualifications.

The next phase can use these packets to assess conventional business quality, deployment credibility, incremental capture and valuation together. Before comparing numerical upside, align earnings periods and tax bases, isolate existing AI in the baseline, identify incremental costs and capital, and refresh price and share-count inputs at the decision date. Company-specific open questions should constrain any subsequent scenario assumptions.

No new comparative ranking, numerical score, target price, portfolio weight or detailed bear/base/bull case has been created in this coverage phase. The original universe scores and first-batch valuation work remain historical inputs, not the final shortlist.
