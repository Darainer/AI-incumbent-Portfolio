# Research roadmap

## Current release

The industry framework and initial 100-company universe are complete as a research starting point. Company proof and valuation remain deliberately open. The deck communicates the hypothesis and the evidence standard without implying a finished fund or track record.

## Next: complete the first 18 company packets

Begin with professional workflows because pricing and renewal evidence can arrive faster than clinical outcomes. In parallel workstreams if resources are assigned, examine risk decisions and physical operations, while building the slower pharma cohort history. This is a suggested work sequence, not an instruction to run unattended agents or a scheduled task.

For each name, close the same gaps: current business fundamentals, actual usable assets and rights, production scope, challenger comparison, measured economics and conventional-business valuation. Record failed hypotheses and all material adverse developments.

## Then: build the underpricing shortlist

Produce at most 10–20 names that pass the business, operating and valuation gates. Fewer is acceptable if the evidence does not justify more. Include dated bear/base/bull valuation, implied expectations, decisive next evidence and a documented rejection case. There is no quota of attractive investments.

## Finally: portfolio design

Assess common suppliers, data regulation, AI-capex exposure, interest rates, business cycles and geographic revenue risks. Only then propose weights, cash reserve, review triggers and position limits. Distinguish existing business risk from the incremental AI option. Backtests, if later requested, must avoid hindsight selection and survivorship bias.

## Release acceptance

The next research release should upgrade evidence rather than merely add names. A credible outcome could be fewer high-conviction candidates, more explicit rejections and a clearer price discipline.
