## Lead acceptance notes

**Allianz (C017):** Financial and insurance-capital anchors support research coverage. AI descriptions remain aggregated and lack reproducibly named production cohorts. E2 should be read as conditional, not a firm evidence promotion; catastrophe and reserve effects require separation.

**Progressive (C019):** Snapshot establishes deployed telematics. The 10-K supports broad longstanding ML/AI, while Photo Estimate remains digital self-service without AI attribution. The USD526.7m underwriting-scale hurdle is a constructed ratio/premium proxy, not reported underwriting profit.

**AIA Group (C020):** AIA reports claims-process adoption and accuracy metrics, but bundled redesign and missing controls prevent AI-only attribution. OPAT is after tax; VONB is a present value and stays separate. Retention of savings under claims/service quality constraints remains unproved.

**BlackRock (C021):** Aladdin distribution and a named client implementation support scoped use. Availability across clients is not active adoption, and technology fees must be separated from market-driven AUM growth and acquisition effects.

**Manulife (C022):** Core earnings are an adjusted after-tax basis; claims and capital effects require separate controls. Underwriting/service mechanisms remain conditional on reproducible deployment and fair, risk-adjusted outcomes.

**DBS Group (C024):** Fraud and credit decision support are plausible bank mechanisms, but the retrieved deployment evidence is limited. Exact-year earnings and tax treatment are aligned; fixed-risk loss outcomes and retained contribution remain open.

**ICICI Bank (C025):** The current standalone bank result is distinct from an incremental AI thesis. Digital-assistant access does not prove generative functionality or fixed-risk credit lift; the quarterly after-tax screen is not an annual savings comparison.

**Mastercard (C027):** The filed payment result supports conventional scale. Fraud-scoring capability needs a matched false-decline/loss comparison and Mastercard-retained economics. The quarterly net-income screen is not comparable with annual pre-tax savings.

**Adyen (C028):** Issuer-reported 0.9 percentage-point conversion improvement supports E2 only. EUR641.5m H1 EBITDA is confirmed. Correct own-contribution illustration is negative EUR19.28m; no positive materiality conclusion is accepted.

**S&P Global (C029):** Filed financials support the conventional business; product material supports a data/research capability but no independently verified production cohort. Upstream licenses, interface substitution and seat economics constrain the thesis.

Acceptance checks source support, scope, materiality arithmetic and explicit limits. It is neither an audit opinion nor a buy/sell recommendation. Raw capsules preserve the researcher's handoff; lead notes govern unresolved differences.
