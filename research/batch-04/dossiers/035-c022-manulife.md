## Provisional conclusion

Manulife has a durable conventional franchise based on policy histories, claims outcomes, adviser distribution and regulated administration. The public evidence supports specific AI capabilities and, in some cases, management-reported operational use. It does not provide a controlled, quality-adjusted company result tied to retained incremental cash. The central underwriting issue is capture: operational improvement may pass to customers, be absorbed by implementation, already sit in the reported baseline, or be dominated by cycle and mix. The dossier therefore keeps G2 provisional and treats E3/E4 as unestablished.

## Conventional business and financial anchor

Manulife reports Asia, Canada, United States, and Global Wealth and Asset Management. Customer value arises from dependable execution, integration and domain expertise, not data volume alone. Competitive advantage must survive Sun Life and digital insurers and wealth platforms using third-party models. Switching costs and distribution help, but contracts, interoperability and procurement can reallocate gains.

For FY2025, company reporting identifies **C$7.5bn core earnings**. Core earnings is a non-GAAP, after-tax-style performance measure and governs this packet’s hurdle; reported shareholder net income must remain separate. Insurance liabilities, LICAT capital, market effects and experience gains/losses make industrial-company comparisons inappropriate.

The [Q2 2026 issuer results page](https://www.manulife.com/ca/en/about-us/investors/results-and-reports) reports quarterly net income attributed to shareholders of C$2.110bn, core earnings of C$1.923bn, core ROE of 16.3%, book value per common share of C$27.48 and financial leverage ratio of 22.2%. Core measures are non-GAAP and the quarter is not annualized.

The ordinary baseline already contains deployed analytics, automation and machine learning. Capital allocation must fund maintenance, growth investment, obligations and shareholder distributions before any incremental AI benefit. Financial scale permits experimentation, but it does not demonstrate quality, adoption or capture.

## Measurement contract and retained economics

Pre-register **vintage loss/claims outcome at fixed mix, expense per resolved case, lapse rate and risk-adjusted new-business value**. Compare randomized or carefully matched sites, clients, products or cohorts against the current production system, including existing ML. Define the eligible workload before rollout; hold service, safety, accuracy, risk and customer-mix quality constant. Report sample size, duration, confidence interval, adoption, exception/rework rate and all human/model/implementation cost. The known public result is insufficient for E3. A future annual report, product cohort study or customer renewal disclosure linking the KPI to contribution could close the gap.

The framework's annual research convention is **C$375m**: 5% × FY2025 core earnings of C$7.5bn, an after-tax-style non-GAAP measure. The illustrative pre-tax bridge is C$2.5bn eligible expense ×10%×60% realization×50% retention−C$40m = C$35m; applying an explicit 20% research tax assumption gives **C$28m after tax**, below the hurdle. Claims avoided require matched risk cohorts.

## Competing hypotheses, falsifiers and gates

**Strongest positive hypothesis:** proprietary workflow context and distribution let Manulife deploy validated AI faster than standalone vendors, with better feedback and a retained service, pricing, throughput or capital benefit. **Strongest negative hypothesis:** model capability commoditizes, customer rights prevent pooling, implementation and verification absorb gains, and Sun Life or digital insurers and wealth platforms using third-party models forces pass-through.

Three falsifiers are: (1) matched deployments fail to improve vintage loss/claims outcome at fixed mix, expense per resolved case, lapse rate and risk-adjusted new-business value at equal quality; (2) five-year retained benefit after recurring AI and implementation cost remains below the stated 5% hurdle; (3) contracts, portability or competitor performance show that the alleged data/workflow advantage is readily replicated. Unresolved questions are eligible-workload share, exact production penetration, customer-level retention/pricing, recurring model cost, contract training rights and the latest post-Q2 2026 adverse development.

G0 passes because asset, mechanism, capture route and falsifiers are explicit. G1 is provisionally supported by the conventional franchise and financial capacity, subject to latest-period refresh and sector risks. G2 remains **open**: scoped E1/E2 can support further testing, but E3/E4 are absent. G3 and G4 are **deferred by user**. Confidence is **medium** for mechanism, **low-to-medium** for deployment breadth and **low** for capture.
