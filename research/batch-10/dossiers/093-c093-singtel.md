# Singtel — evidence packet

**Identity.** Singapore Telecommunications Limited, SGX:Z74, SGD. Cutoff 12 September 2026; latest reported period Q1 FY2027 ended June 2026; FY2026 remains the annual materiality anchor. AI exposure is scoped to sovereign GPU/cloud infrastructure, network orchestration and NCS enterprise implementation. Conventional connectivity and generic data-center growth are kept separate where disclosure permits. G3/G4 are deferred.

## Business and financial baseline

Singtel combines Singapore and Australian telecommunications, NCS technology services, Digital InfraCo/Nxera data centers and regional associates including Airtel and AIS. Its scarce assets are spectrum, networks, enterprise relationships, regulated local presence, sites and regional connectivity. Hyperscalers, regional data-center operators, telcos and systems integrators compete. NVIDIA, model vendors and energy suppliers can capture a large share of AI economics.

FY2026 operating revenue was **S$14.261bn**, EBITDA **S$3.848bn**, OpCo EBIT **S$1.504bn**, share of associates' pre-tax profit S$2.887bn, underlying net profit **S$2.769bn** and reported net profit S$5.606bn. [Singtel financial summary](https://www.singtel.com/about-us/investor-relations/financial-summary). The gap reflects exceptional items, mainly stake sales, so reported net profit is unsuitable for recurring materiality. FY2026 slides show **S$8.7bn net debt**, 1.3× net debt to EBITDA plus associate PBT, 19× interest cover and stable free cash flow despite higher Tuas data-center capex. [FY2026 slides](https://cdn1.singteldigital.com/content/dam/singtel/investorRelations/financialResults/2026/h2fy26/FY26_slides_SGX_filing.pdf). Singtel’s 13 August Q1 FY2027 business update reported operating revenue **S$3.558bn**, underlying net profit **S$831m** and reported net profit **S$818m**. The exact SGX announcement page returned an error in this retrieval, so these latest-period figures are recorded as issuer-filed but not used in arithmetic. [SGX announcement](https://links.sgx.com/1.0.0/corporate-announcements/4K16A58L1LIYSHN4/). OpCo EBIT is used as a pre-tax operating anchor; associate economics and asset recycling remain outside the AI bridge.

## Mechanisms, deployment and rights

**RE:AI sovereign cloud and GPU as a service.** Singtel describes RE:AI as combining Nxera data centers, Paragon network/cloud orchestration and partners including NVIDIA, Mistral, Scale AI and Nscale. It offers GPU capacity, models and tooling with control over data location. [Digital InfraCo](https://www.singtel.com/digital-infraco). Customer workloads consume reserved or on-demand capacity; Singtel earns infrastructure/service revenue; retained value is revenue less power, hardware depreciation/financing, software, support and partner shares. The product is E2: publicly offered infrastructure. No disclosed utilization, contracted backlog, unit economics or AI-specific revenue supports E3.

**Nxera AI-ready data centers.** Singtel states more than 400 MW is in operation and under development across Asian markets and highlights liquid cooling. The combined figure is not live AI capacity, and capacity is not demand. A valid result requires commissioned MW, energized GPU racks, contracted/used capacity, revenue per MW, power-usage effectiveness and return on invested capital. General colocation tenants or capacity under development should not be attributed to AI.

**Paragon orchestration.** The platform can place workloads across edge, network and cloud with assured quality of service. The causal path is orchestration to lower latency/operational complexity, higher enterprise adoption and recurring platform/connectivity revenue. Awards and platform availability do not demonstrate renewal or contribution profit.

**NCS enterprise AI.** NCS can implement models and workflows for regulated Asian enterprises and governments. Existing relationships and local delivery help distribution; project services can be labor-intensive and lower-margin. Value should be measured as recurring managed-service gross profit, renewal and delivery hours after model/vendor cost, not bookings labeled AI.

Customer prompts, documents and models remain customer-controlled under enterprise contracts. Data-residency capability can reduce a barrier but does not transfer training rights to Singtel. Telecom network telemetry is controlled by the operator but subject to communications secrecy and privacy. GPU vendors constrain hardware access and pricing; model partners control weights/licenses. Joint-venture ownership of regional data centers and associate structures further split economics. These rights and partner shares make gross customer productivity an invalid proxy for Singtel cash.

## KPI contract and materiality

Primary contract: **AI infrastructure contribution profit per commissioned MW**, reported quarterly as contracted and consumed GPU-hours × realized price, less power, hardware depreciation/lease, model/software, operations and partner shares. Include commissioned MW, utilization, backlog duration, customer concentration and capex. For NCS, report recurring AI managed-service revenue and contribution margin, renewal, implementation hours and failures. For Paragon, report paying workloads and net recurring revenue rather than endpoints enabled.

Five percent of FY2026 OpCo EBIT is **S$75.2m/year**. Illustration: 100 commissioned AI MW × S$2.5m annual revenue/MW × 65% utilization × 45% contribution margin × 70% Singtel retention = **S$51.19m**. Add S$500m eligible NCS revenue ×10% incremental AI share ×25% contribution margin ×70% retention = **S$8.75m**; subtract S$25m recurring platform/control expense, net **S$34.94m/year**. Required infrastructure utilization holding other assumptions is `(75.2+25−8.75)/(100×2.5×.45×.70)=116.1%`, infeasible. Inputs are scenarios, not guidance. Both bridge and hurdle are pre-tax; customer productivity is excluded.

Positive: local regulated relationships, connectivity and data-residency infrastructure create distribution. Negative: capital intensity, power constraints, underutilization, hyperscaler competition and vendor shares can erase returns. Falsifiers: commissioned capacity lacks contracts; utilization or price falls below plan; PUE/energy cost compresses margin; NCS work stays non-recurring; returns trail cost of capital. Questions: live AI MW, contracted GPU hours, partner economics, recurring NCS revenue, capex and data rights.

Confidence mechanism high, deployment medium-high, capture low. G0 pass; G1 provisional with manageable disclosed leverage but large capex; G2 insufficient because product/capacity evidence is E2 without AI-specific unit economics or retained cash. G3/G4 deferred.
