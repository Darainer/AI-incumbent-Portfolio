## Provisional conclusion and conventional business

Iberdrola has a credible incumbent-AI mechanism because it controls regulated grid topology, smart-meter/asset telemetry, outage history, renewable fleet and dispatch rights. The scoped evidence assessment is **E1 public AI program/capability; no opened material deployment cohort**. It does **not** establish controlled quality-adjusted improvement across a representative workload or incremental cash retained by shareholders. The investment question therefore remains operational: can management disclose a stable baseline, eligible workload, quality guardrail, realized economics and recurring technology cost?

The latest conventional anchor is [H1 2026 (ended 30 June 2026)](https://www.iberdrola.com/shareholders-investors/operational-financial-information): **regulated/network and generation revenue was not reliably extracted from the opened summary; Q1 sales were €12.017bn** and **H1 reported net profit €4.34bn, up 22%; adjusted profit context is needed because transactions/tax timing matter**. These figures are reported unless explicitly labelled adjusted. utility cash flow must be considered after network/renewables capex; Q1 disclosed €14.5bn investment over the preceding twelve months. Q1 adjusted net debt about €50.3bn; regulated returns, rates and construction commitments dominate financing. The principal confounders are cycle, mix, pricing, acquisitions/restructuring, working capital and capital intensity. Existing prediction, optimization and automation are already in these reported results; only incremental improvement from here belongs in an AI bridge.

## AI mechanisms and competitive challenge

**Mechanism 1 — scoped workflow improvement.** predictive maintenance and vegetation/fault analytics can lower outages and truck rolls at equal safety and reliability.

**Mechanism 2 — execution or adjacent demand.** forecasting can improve renewable dispatch, balancing and storage; regulated sharing and market prices determine shareholder retention.

**Challenger and substitute.** Enel, National Grid and local DSOs have similar assets; equipment vendors and grid-software suppliers can capture the analytics layer.

**Adverse evidence.** regulated allowances may pass savings to customers; €50bn-plus debt and €14.5bn investment intensity make small opex savings secondary to capex execution and allowed returns.

## Measurement contract and materiality arithmetic

The framework’s research convention is **€434m: 5% × (2 × H1 2026 reported net profit of €4.34bn), an annualized reported-profit proxy rather than normalized earnings**. This is a scale test, not a forecast or valuation.

| KPI / unit | Baseline and comparator | Eligible workload | Quality condition | Known result / next proof event |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Outage minutes/customer | matched feeder/weather cohort | AI-maintained feeders | same safety and capex condition | regulator-audited feeder cohort |
| Balancing cost/MWh | existing forecast/dispatch | AI-forecast renewable MWh | same curtailment and reliability | market cohort plus regulated sharing |

Illustrative cost bridge: `€5bn eligible grid operations, maintenance and loss/outage cost × 8% task efficiency × 40% realization × 50% retained share − €100m recurring AI expense = negative €20m annual operating benefit`. All bridge inputs are assumptions. The result is below the hurdle. It excludes customer savings; any revenue case must use incremental company contribution after cannibalization, servicing and model cost. Capex and working capital remain separate.

## Hypotheses, falsifiers and evidence gates

**Strongest positive hypothesis:** the installed asset and distribution let Iberdrola embed models in a difficult workflow, observe outcomes and retain part of better throughput, decisions or service. **Strongest negative hypothesis:** generic models and rivals reach the same customer context, contractual rights block pooled learning, and customers, workers or suppliers capture the gain while implementation/capital cost remains with the company.

Three falsifiers: (1) by the next two annual reporting cycles, management still cannot disclose active production penetration and a quality-adjusted KPI for a material workload; (2) renewals, usage or contribution margin deteriorate as AI pricing, cannibalization and supplier costs rise; (3) matched rollouts fail to beat the existing production system after errors, rework, safety/risk and implementation cost. Core-business deterioration must be diagnosed separately from AI failure.

Unresolved questions are the precise customer-data licenses; whether feedback may improve a pooled model; eligible workload; representative baseline; error and escalation rate; incremental price/volume; labor realization; recurring inference/support cost; required capex; and the share of savings retained after competition or regulation.

**G0:** provisionally satisfied: asset, mechanisms, capture hypothesis and falsifiers are explicit. **G1:** provisionally satisfied only subject to the stated balance-sheet, governance and rights gaps. **G2:** provisional **watch / incomplete**: E1 public AI program/capability; no opened material deployment cohort; E3 quality-adjusted evidence and E4 retained cash are absent. **G3/G4:** deferred by user. Confidence is **moderate** in mechanism, **low-to-moderate** in broad deployment and **low** in capture.
