## Lead acceptance notes

**Costco (C091):** Costco’s operating business is documented, but an AI thesis was not established from the opened sources. The incomplete-year screen is not annualized; ordinary merchandising and membership economics remain the baseline.

**Deutsche Telekom (C092):** An announced AI rollout is E1. Network, field-service and contact-centre improvements need separate deployment and service-quality proof. The quarterly net-profit screen is after tax and not comparable with annual pre-tax savings.

**Singtel (C093):** RE:AI is commercial offering/capacity evidence, not demonstrated occupied AI capacity or investment returns. Operating and development MW stay separate. Latest-quarter access limits and the infeasible utilization illustration remain explicit.

**Meta Platforms (C094):** The 22% advertiser ROAS uplift is an issuer claim. The inaccessible protocol does not establish randomization of the AI-versus-baseline contrast, so E3 is withheld. Advertiser revenue cannot be treated as Meta revenue; capex and retained contribution remain decisive.

**Netflix (C095):** Recommendation ML is an established baseline, not a new incremental benefit. New generative tools require separate retention, advertising or production-cost tests; the annual operating scale alone establishes no AI capture.

**Shell (C096):** AI use cases and predictive maintenance are plausible, with source-access limits. Adjusted earnings remain an after-tax non-GAAP scale. Commodity, trading and project effects prevent aggregate AI attribution.

**SLB (C097):** The corrected issuer release supports current pretax profit and cash flow. Lumi/product deployment remains a limited-source hypothesis. Oilfield demand and separate data-centre demand do not establish internal AI productivity.

**Iberdrola (C098):** The issuer corroborates H1 reported net profit. Regulated cost sharing, investments and transaction effects govern owner capture. Named outage/safety-adjusted AI outcomes remain unverified; the interim net-profit screen is after tax.

**Prologis (C099):** The filed release corroborates core FFO per share. A named owner AI deployment was not established. Aggregate cost assumptions cannot be compared with a per-share FFO hurdle without a consistent share count and period.

**CBRE Group (C100):** Ellis supports named research, contract and sales-assistant use. Management time-saving claims lack controls and cash reconciliation. Pass-through client expenses, fee terms and real-estate cyclicality govern retained contribution.

Acceptance checks source support, scope, materiality arithmetic and explicit limits. It is neither an audit opinion nor a buy/sell recommendation. Raw capsules preserve the researcher's handoff; lead notes govern unresolved differences.
