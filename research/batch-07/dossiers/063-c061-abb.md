# ABB — evidence packet

**Identity.** ABB Ltd registered shares, SIX:ABBN (CHF quote) and NYSE:ABBNY ADR; reporting USD. Cutoff 12 September 2026; latest Q2 2026 released 16 July. Unreviewed; G3/G4 deferred. ABB has deployed industrial analytics/AI products and a large installed base, but advertised customer savings are best-case/vendor claims without controlled denominators or ABB cash capture.

## Business/financial anchor

ABB provides Electrification, Motion and Automation products/software/services. Installed drives, motors, switchgear and control systems create service relationships; channel reach and safety-critical integration matter. Siemens, Schneider and Rockwell compete. FY2025 revenue **$33.22bn**, operational EBITA margin **19.0%** and orders $36.77bn; a $2bn buyback was launched. Q2 2026 revenue **$9.47bn**, reported operating margin **16.7%**, operational EBITA **$1.93bn**, orders $12.04bn. The proposed $5.5bn Rotork acquisition and 2025 $4.8bn robotics disposal materially change mix/capital. [ABB Q2 materials](https://global.abb/group/en/investors/results-and-presentations/quarterly-results), 16 July 2026. AI-data-center electrification demand is separate from ABB’s internal/customer productivity.

## Mechanisms/competition

**Genix predictive maintenance/optimization (classical ML):** telemetry → anomaly/remaining-life prediction → less downtime/energy at equal quality → software/service revenue. Product is deployed/offered (E2), but “up to” 40% O&M reductions are vendor-selected claims, not controlled E3. **Copilot/RAG assistance (generative):** manuals and asset context → troubleshooting/action drafts → technician time; safety requires verification. **Energy/production optimization:** process data → setpoint suggestions → energy/tonne and throughput, but customer receives most savings and existing control is baseline.

Siemens and Schneider are incumbent challengers; hyperscaler/independent historians and customer teams are substitutes. Rotork purchase may improve valve data/distribution but is not an AI result.

### Attribution, realization and capture

Predictive-maintenance attribution needs an asset-level prospective design. Eligible motors, drives or process trains should be assigned in phases, stratified by age, duty cycle and site. The comparison must count false alerts, planned interventions, spare parts, technician travel and production conditions. A model can look accurate while destroying value if it pulls maintenance forward unnecessarily. Conversely, an avoided failure estimate based only on the model's own counterfactual is circular. Closure requires actual failure and downtime outcomes with an independently specified baseline.

Energy optimization should use metered kWh, steam or fuel per specification-conforming tonne, with weather, feedstock, product mix and utilization controls. Energy-price changes are not physical productivity. Closed-loop setpoint changes also require process-safety review, so recommendation acceptance and operator override rates belong in the disclosure. ABB can benefit through paid software, service renewals, equipment pull-through or lower warranty/service cost. Customer energy and labor savings belong to the customer until contract pricing or renewal shows ABB captured them.

Genix's “up to” reduction language is weak evidence because the population, baseline and selection rule are not public. The CrateDB case supports a production data architecture, not ABB's incremental economics. Likewise, the external RAG study uses ABB manuals but is not an ABB production deployment. It is useful counterevidence about hallucination and completeness risk only. These ownership boundaries prevent vendor, academic and customer claims from being imported into ABB's G2 assessment.

Data access is contract-specific. A customer may permit inference within its tenant while prohibiting pooled training. Equipment makers, historians, systems integrators and cloud providers can each possess overlapping telemetry. ABB's strongest defensibility is therefore installed workflow integration, expert service labels and safety validation. Model weights alone are unlikely to be scarce. If interfaces become open and customers insist on data portability, switching costs and retained share could fall.

The Rotork transaction and robotics disposal create a moving perimeter. Any future growth in digital revenue, installed base or margin must be restated to a comparable perimeter. Data-center electrification demand is a separate product-cycle tailwind and cannot prove that Genix or copilots improved customer productivity. A credible finance bridge would reconcile paid attach, gross margin, recurring cloud/support cost, acquisition effects and working capital to retained operating cash.

| KPI | Comparator/quality | Known | Closure |

Annual 5% hurdle using FY25 operational EBITA proxy: `$33.22bn×19%×5%=$315.6m`. Illustration: $5bn eligible digital/service revenue×8% uplift×35% adoption×60% margin×75% retention=$63m; $10bn eligible cost×5% efficiency×35% realization×60% retention−$50m cost=$55m; total **$118m/year**. Required revenue uplift `(315.6-55)/(5000×.35×.6×.75)=33.1%`. All assumptions; customer savings are not ABB costs.

Positive: installed equipment/context let ABB deliver closed-loop gains and sell service. Negative: customers own data/savings, rivals replicate analytics, hardware cycle/data-center demand explain results. Falsifiers: no paid attach/renewal; matched downtime/energy fails; model/support cost consumes margin. Questions: connected assets, rights, pricing, controlled results, Rotork debt.

Confidence mechanism high, deployment medium, capture low-medium. G0/G1 provisional pass; G2 provisional/insufficient: E2 products, no E3/E4 ABB result. G3/G4 deferred.

The minimum disclosure that would change the assessment is: connected assets as a share of the eligible fleet; paid Genix annual recurring revenue and renewal; matched downtime and energy results with quality and safety controls; alert precision and intervention cost; customer-data reuse terms; and contribution after cloud, partner and field-support expense. Without those items, deployment is credible but group materiality and durable capture are not demonstrated.
