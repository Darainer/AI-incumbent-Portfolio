## Provisional conclusion and conventional business

Canadian National Railway has a credible incumbent-AI mechanism because it controls rights-of-way, terminals, train/wayside telemetry, shipment flows and operating rules. The scoped evidence assessment is **E1 incremental AI; inspection/optimization is ordinary baseline**. It does **not** establish controlled quality-adjusted improvement across a representative workload or incremental cash retained by shareholders. The investment question therefore remains operational: can management disclose a stable baseline, eligible workload, quality guardrail, realized economics and recurring technology cost?

The latest conventional anchor is [Q2 2026 (ended 30 June 2026)](https://www.cn.ca/en/investors/financial-results/): **C$4.753bn revenue** and **C$1.781bn operating income, up 9%**. These figures are reported unless explicitly labelled adjusted. C$1.84bn free cash flow in H1 2026, up 19%. railroad debt, leases and pension claims support a long-lived network; track and locomotive capex are unavoidable. The principal confounders are cycle, mix, pricing, acquisitions/restructuring, working capital and capital intensity. Existing prediction, optimization and automation are already in these reported results; only incremental improvement from here belongs in an AI bridge.

Public materials establish workflow access but not unrestricted pooled learning; rights remain product-, customer- and jurisdiction-specific.

## AI mechanisms and competitive challenge

**Mechanism 1 — scoped workflow improvement.** predictive inspection can identify wheel/track faults and schedule maintenance; safety performance and false negatives are essential quality conditions.

**Mechanism 2 — execution or adjacent demand.** train planning and yard optimization can improve velocity, dwell and fuel use; existing optimization and volume/mix remain in baseline.

**Challenger and substitute.** CPKC has a competing continental network; trucks and intermodal alternatives constrain service pricing.

**Adverse evidence.** weather, labor, commodity mix and network capital explain much quarterly variation; opaque safety algorithms and regulatory requirements limit autonomous execution.

## Measurement contract and materiality arithmetic

The framework’s research convention is **C$350m, roughly 5% of a C$7.1bn annualized Q2 operating-income proxy**. This is a scale test, not a forecast or valuation.

| KPI / unit | Baseline and comparator | Eligible workload | Quality condition | Known result / next proof event |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Train speed/dwell at fixed safety | matched corridor/season | optimized trains/yards | same accidents, defects and service | corridor cohort controlling mix/weather |
| Failures per million car-miles | current inspection regime | AI-inspected equipment | same false negatives and removals | audited detector cohort |

Illustrative cost bridge: `C$5bn eligible annual labor, fuel and equipment cost × 6% task efficiency × 40% realization × 60% retained share − C$50m recurring AI expense = C$22m annual operating benefit`. All bridge inputs are assumptions. The result is below the hurdle. It excludes customer savings; any revenue case must use incremental company contribution after cannibalization, servicing and model cost. Capex and working capital remain separate.

## Hypotheses, falsifiers and evidence gates

**Strongest positive hypothesis:** the installed asset and distribution let Canadian National Railway embed models in a difficult workflow, observe outcomes and retain part of better throughput, decisions or service. **Strongest negative hypothesis:** generic models and rivals reach the same customer context, contractual rights block pooled learning, and customers, workers or suppliers capture the gain while implementation/capital cost remains with the company.

Three falsifiers: (1) by the next two annual reporting cycles, management still cannot disclose active production penetration and a quality-adjusted KPI for a material workload; (2) renewals, usage or contribution margin deteriorate as AI pricing, cannibalization and supplier costs rise; (3) matched rollouts fail to beat the existing production system after errors, rework, safety/risk and implementation cost. Core-business deterioration must be diagnosed separately from AI failure.

Unresolved questions are the precise customer-data licenses; whether feedback may improve a pooled model; eligible workload; representative baseline; error and escalation rate; incremental price/volume; labor realization; recurring inference/support cost; required capex; and the share of savings retained after competition or regulation.

**G0:** provisionally satisfied: asset, mechanisms, capture hypothesis and falsifiers are explicit. **G1:** provisionally satisfied only subject to the stated balance-sheet, governance and rights gaps. **G2:** provisional **watch / incomplete**: E1 incremental AI; inspection/optimization is ordinary baseline; E3 quality-adjusted evidence and E4 retained cash are absent. **G3/G4:** deferred by user. Confidence is **moderate** in mechanism, **low-to-moderate** in broad deployment and **low** in capture.
