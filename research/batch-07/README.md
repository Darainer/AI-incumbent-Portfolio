## Lead acceptance notes

**Palo Alto Networks (C057):** The corrected annual GAAP operating anchor is materially different from adjusted earnings. Cortex/Precision AI capability does not establish agent adoption or error-adjusted containment economics; security quality and customer retention remain open.

**CrowdStrike (C058):** A named Charlotte AI customer supports scoped E2. Current GAAP loss and non-GAAP profit must both remain visible. Investigation speed without detection quality and economic controls cannot establish retained cash.

**ABB (C061):** Genix has an identifiable product and partner route; exact source access is partly limited. Customer plant savings do not become ABB profit without attach, renewal or service contribution. Operational EBITA is explicitly adjusted.

**Caterpillar (C063):** Command has substantial operating scale, but cumulative tonnes and miles are not causal productivity. The corrected filed annual operating-profit anchor governs; per-truck monetization, customer sharing and safety require evidence.

**Komatsu (C064):** AHS and Smart Construction provide management-reported operating workflow evidence. Correct fiscal-year profit is used. Customer fuel/labour gains need a vendor monetization route and cannot be counted directly as Komatsu savings.

**DHL Group (C065):** The annual EBIT anchor is reconciled. Routing and facility automation need a named AI deployment and matched service/quality controls; trend reports alone do not establish production outcomes.

**UPS (C066):** Network transformation and ordinary route optimization form the baseline. Workforce or facility savings are not automatically AI savings. The annual adjusted target is explicitly forward-looking; service reliability and retained gains remain unproved.

**Canadian National Railway (C067):** Digital planning and inspection do not isolate incremental AI. Weather, network capex and traffic mix require controls. The quarterly operating screen is not comparable with annual assumptions.

**Safran (C068):** The financial result is supported, but no named Safran AI deployment was verified. Predictive-maintenance mechanisms remain hypotheses; aftermarket pricing, traffic and CFM/operator rights are central confounders.

**GE Aerospace (C069):** Engine Health Monitoring supports named operating analytics at fleet scale. This is baseline deployment evidence, not isolated incremental AI efficacy. Separate consolidated, adjusted and CES measures and customer savings from GE contribution.

Acceptance checks source support, scope, materiality arithmetic and explicit limits. It is neither an audit opinion nor a buy/sell recommendation. Raw capsules preserve the researcher's handoff; lead notes govern unresolved differences.
