## Lead acceptance notes

**Eli Lilly (C006):** TuneLab uses proprietary research data and daily scientist workflows; factory capacity is an input. Corrected primary sources and aligned annual/NPV/tax arithmetic support the evidence packet, not clinical success or retained cash.

**Siemens Healthineers (C007):** Accept with source-access limit: current Q3 margin and Diagnostics weakness are corroborated; FY2025 EBIT figure was not independently re-extracted by the auditor. AI-Rad establishes available workflow integration; adoption and controlled clinical/cash effects remain open.

**Medtronic (C008):** The GI Genius study supports a narrowly scoped detection outcome, with quasi-randomization, non-blinding and subgroup limits. Non-significant NNRR is not equivalence. Q1 FY2027 freshness and the 65.25% assumed attach calculation are corrected; no group cash inference follows.

**Intuitive Surgical (C009):** AI-powered Case Insights is a bounded available feature, not evidence of clinical benefit or material digital revenue. The 10-K explicitly says digital/learning revenue is not material. USD96.3m benefit is assumption-driven versus USD147.3m operating-profit scale.

**Thermo Fisher Scientific (C010):** The annual operating anchor and current interim now agree. Instrument AI capability needs matched analytical accuracy, downtime and vendor capture; inaccessible product details limit any firm deployment conclusion.

**Danaher (C011):** The governing annual metric is net earnings, not the old operating-profit proxy. The illustrative savings have been tax-aligned. Product-level quality and paid service economics remain unproved.

**IQVIA (C012):** Healthcare AI capability is plausible but a named production cohort is missing. The corrected annual adjusted-EBITDA guidance screen avoids double annualization; backlog and bookings are not earnings.

**HCA Healthcare (C013):** Lead independently verified the final Q2 release after the earlier access failure. Payer mix, directed payments and surgery volumes confound results. Nurse-handoff capability requires omission/escalation and clinical-quality controls.

**UnitedHealth Group (C014):** Optum/Humata outcome claims are now correctly separated from UHG production. Removal of authorization requirements is a policy change. Litigation establishes disputed allegations and discovery, not an adverse merits finding. No inference of UHG-specific AI benefit is accepted.

**Swiss Re (C016):** Impact+ remains E1 capability. The final screen uses USD2.8bn H1 net income and a USD140m same-half after-tax hurdle. Its annual benefit illustration is NOT_COMPARABLE without time alignment. Benign catastrophes and reserve development confound aggregate results.

Acceptance checks source support, scope, materiality arithmetic and explicit limits. It is neither an audit opinion nor a buy/sell recommendation. Raw capsules preserve the researcher's handoff; lead notes govern unresolved differences.
