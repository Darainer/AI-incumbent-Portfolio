## Provisional conclusion and conventional business

IQVIA has a credible incumbent-AI mechanism because it controls licensed health records, claims, prescriptions, trial operations, investigators and longitudinal commercialization data. The scoped evidence assessment is **E1 capability; no opened named production cohort**. It does **not** establish controlled quality-adjusted improvement across a representative workload or incremental cash retained by shareholders. The investment question therefore remains operational: can management disclose a stable baseline, eligible workload, quality guardrail, realized economics and recurring technology cost?

The latest conventional anchor is [Q2 2026 (ended 30 June 2026)](https://ir.iqvia.com/financial-information/quarterly-results/default.aspx): **$4.368bn revenue, up 8.7%** and **adjusted EPS $3.15; R&DS backlog $34.2bn with $9.2bn expected to convert in twelve months**. These figures are reported unless explicitly labelled adjusted. cash conversion and buybacks must be evaluated against acquisition-related intangibles and debt; $398m of shares were repurchased in Q2. leveraged CRO/data model; interest, acquired intangibles and sponsor cancellations are material claims. The principal confounders are cycle, mix, pricing, acquisitions/restructuring, working capital and capital intensity. Existing prediction, optimization and automation are already in these reported results; only incremental improvement from here belongs in an AI bridge.

## AI mechanisms and competitive challenge

**Mechanism 1 — scoped workflow improvement.** Healthcare-grade AI can assist site selection, patient matching, document review and trial monitoring; the relevant output is faster quality-adjusted enrollment or lower cost per completed milestone.

**Mechanism 2 — execution or adjacent demand.** commercial analytics can use licensed longitudinal data to improve targeting and evidence generation; classical analytics is already embedded in reported results.

**Challenger and substitute.** ICON and Parexel contest CRO execution; Veeva, Komodo and customer-owned data platforms can unbundle analytics and workflow.

**Adverse evidence.** Bookings and backlog are not realized margin, sponsor biotech funding can change quickly, and privacy/contract restrictions limit pooled learning from patient/customer data.

## Hypotheses, falsifiers and evidence gates

**Strongest positive hypothesis:** the installed asset and distribution let IQVIA embed models in a difficult workflow, observe outcomes and retain part of better throughput, decisions or service. **Strongest negative hypothesis:** generic models and rivals reach the same customer context, contractual rights block pooled learning, and customers, workers or suppliers capture the gain while implementation/capital cost remains with the company.

Three falsifiers: (1) by the next two annual reporting cycles, management still cannot disclose active production penetration and a quality-adjusted KPI for a material workload; (2) renewals, usage or contribution margin deteriorate as AI pricing, cannibalization and supplier costs rise; (3) matched rollouts fail to beat the existing production system after errors, rework, safety/risk and implementation cost. Core-business deterioration must be diagnosed separately from AI failure.

Unresolved questions are the precise customer-data licenses; whether feedback may improve a pooled model; eligible workload; representative baseline; error and escalation rate; incremental price/volume; labor realization; recurring inference/support cost; required capex; and the share of savings retained after competition or regulation.

**G0:** provisionally satisfied: asset, mechanisms, capture hypothesis and falsifiers are explicit. **G1:** provisionally satisfied only subject to the stated balance-sheet, governance and rights gaps. **G2:** provisional **watch / incomplete**: E1 capability; no opened named production cohort; E3 quality-adjusted evidence and E4 retained cash are absent. **G3/G4:** deferred by user. Confidence is **moderate** in mechanism, **low-to-moderate** in broad deployment and **low** in capture.
