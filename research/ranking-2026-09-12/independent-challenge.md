# Independent challenge — incumbent AI candidates

12 September 2026 · Comparative operating judgment after reviewing all 100 · No current-price ranking

**RELX, Intuit, Thomson Reuters, Wolters Kluwer and Veeva are my leading incumbent-workflow candidates. ServiceNow and MercadoLibre deserve serious consideration alongside them.** The strongest common feature is an existing paid route from information to consequential action. A giant database, a model announcement or AI infrastructure demand is less persuasive. Microsoft and Meta have exceptional distribution and credible deployment, but their capital allocation makes incremental shareholder capture a materially harder underwriting problem.

This judgment uses the [research standard](../../framework/research-standard.md), [scoring rules](../../framework/scoring.md), [valuation framework](../../framework/valuation.md), [coverage synthesis](../coverage-synthesis.md), all 90 additional capsules and their [lead review](../coverage-review.json), and the [first-ten lead judgment](../batch-01/lead-review.md). I read underlying dossiers where workflow, evidence scope or capital treatment affected selection. Five decisive primary documents were independently reopened; a sixth failed to render, as recorded below. This is an independent challenge memo, not a re-audit of every source. Preserved universe scores were not inputs to the ordering.

The ordering below expresses **regime fit and underwriting priority**, without claiming attractive expected returns at current prices. Bands are more defensible than adjacent positions. A candidate with unresolved G2 evidence is explicitly conditional; ranking cannot waive its gate. Conversely, G2 does not demand E3 or E4 already exist. A credible E2 deployment and route to material, measurable net benefit can support an explicitly assumed scenario.

## Primary-source challenges reopened in this pass

**RELX — preserve the denominator.** The 23 July H1 transcript, pp. 7–9, reports roughly 90% of new-sales **value** and three-quarters of renewal **value** in the AI-enabled legal package. These are not 90% and 75% incremental growth, full-base penetration or a causal AI premium. Management also describes users/usage outgrowing institutions/value. Token cost below 1% of group cost does not include all development and review work. This strengthens commercial deployment while limiting the profit inference. [RELX H1 transcript](https://www.relx.com/~/media/Files/R/RELX-Group/documents/investors/transcripts/first-half-results-2026-transcript.pdf).

**Thomson Reuters — narrow the alleged learning loop.** The AI FAQ, accessed 12 September, explicitly excludes user content and prompts from training/improving CoCounsel and the underlying models, and constrains third-party partners. The franchise can still monetize editorial content, workflow and customer-specific retrieval. A bull case founded on unrestricted reuse of confidential customer work is inconsistent with this policy. [Thomson Reuters AI FAQ](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en/artificial-intelligence).

**Veeva — distinguish deployment from plans.** The 26 August release's highlights identify a top-20 biopharma's full U.S. field-team deployment of Vault CRM and Agentic Call Report. The customer is not publicly named there. This is narrower but stronger than mere availability. More than 180 live Vault CRM customers are not 180 verified AI deployments; Falcon's five early adopters still precede the stated initial go-lives. This confirms the lead's E2 nuance, not an E3 upgrade. [Veeva Q2 FY2027 results](https://ir.veeva.com/news/news-details/2026/Veeva-Announces-Fiscal-2027-Second-Quarter-Results/default.aspx).

**MercadoLibre — retain the positive evidence as well as the limitation.** The 5 August release, pp. 1 and 3, says search rollout covered its five largest sites and conversion/click-through gains more than offset third-party LLM costs. The capsule's wording should not obscure that positive management claim. It supplies no uplift magnitude or full cost attribution. The same release attributes stronger credit quality partly to moving toward lower-risk borrowers; it does not prove AI improved losses at fixed risk. [MercadoLibre Q2 release](https://investor.mercadolibre.com/open-file?file=aHR0cHM6Ly9odHRwMi5tbHN0YXRpYy5jb20vc3RvcmFnZS9tbC1jbXMtYmFja2VuZC9jbXMtZG9jdW1lbnRzLXByb2QvNWRiYmE5MTktNzIxYy00OTE2LWJiOTEtYzc2YTg3MTNmN2YwL2JkNjBhMzQyLWM1MjMtNDFiOC1hZjdhLWRhMjgxOGZlZjVmZi9NRUxJX1EyXzIwMjZfUHJlc3NfUmVsZWFzZS5wZGY).

**Meta — reconcile capitalization with the earnings narrative.** The 29 July release confirms Q2 operating cash of $31.862bn and capex plus finance-lease principal of $31.078bn, producing $784m FCF. H1 FCF was $13.170bn, so mechanically annualizing Q2 would also mislead. Full-year capex guidance is $130–145bn. A bullish earnings scenario needs cash-return assumptions consistent with that investment, including later depreciation and replacement capital. Low current cash flow alone does not establish poor project returns. [Meta Q2 results and cash-flow reconciliation](https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/Meta-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx).

**ServiceNow — attempted, not independently reproduced.** The [Q2 release](https://newsroom.servicenow.com/press-releases/details/2026/ServiceNow-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/) returned an internal rendering error in this pass. I retain the prior lead's supported E2 and ACV qualification; I do not claim a fresh verification or infer non-deployment from an access failure. ## Hard objections the final shortlist must answer

1. **The current cash-flow baseline already contains substantial AI.** RELX analytics, Experian scores, Meta recommendations, Progressive pricing, EDA optimization and Cat autonomy must not receive their historical value a second time. The bull increment starts after the latest normalized baseline.
2. **A workflow monopoly is narrower than a customer-data monopoly.** TR explicitly rejects the customer-training story. Veeva, SAP, Intuit and LSEG can retain workflow value without pooled learning, but an external authorized agent can change which layer sets price. Model that bargaining outcome instead of assuming it away.
3. **All-software upside is internally inconsistent.** If agents remove many professional seats, RELX/TR/WK, Salesforce, ServiceNow, SAP and ADP cannot each receive full labor-replacement upside while all per-seat bases grow unchanged. Use account, task or consumption economics and model the same labor shock consistently.
4. **Task savings are not an acceptable bull-case profit input.** Required Veeva upsell, ASML software uplift, Cat value per truck and dsm cycle reduction are scale tests built from assumptions. They are not evidence that those levels are achievable. Prefer bounded non-overlapping revenue and cost bridges.
5. **Capex and investment gains cannot disappear when the valuation method changes.** Microsoft and Meta require incremental capital returns; MELI requires credit funding and logistics cash; Synopsys requires acquisition debt/dilution treatment. Microsoft, Alphabet and Salesforce also require explicit normalization of strategic-investment gains before an earnings multiple is used. An EPS multiple does not resolve investment obligations, while treating every growth-capex dollar as permanent maintenance would also misstate economics.
6. **Cheap-looking incumbents need impairment cases.** WKL could preserve tax/compliance value while clinical answers commoditize. Intuit could gain agent activity while losing tax units or expert revenue. A low multiple must be tested against those segment paths before calling AI optionality free.
7. **Insurance upside needs a capital- and cycle-consistent denominator.** Keep catastrophe luck, reserve release and lower-risk mix out of AI attribution. Chubb/Progressive base cases get no separate AI benefit here. Life value-of-new-business and programme NPV cannot be added to annual insurer earnings.
8. **Evidence gaps and negative evidence are different.** KLA, Mastercard, ADP and S&P are conditional because the retrieved evidence is thin, not because AI has failed. WKL has actual bounded adverse comparison evidence. The final ranking should preserve that distinction and avoid disguising uneven research depth as a calibrated posterior score.

My strongest positive challenge to a conservative shortlist is to include **Veeva and MercadoLibre** alongside the first-batch favorites. My strongest negative challenge is to resist automatically promoting **EDA, hyperscalers, insurance and pharmaceutical data scale** into high incremental-AI profit assumptions. No price, target, return forecast, portfolio weight or G4 promotion is made in this memo.
