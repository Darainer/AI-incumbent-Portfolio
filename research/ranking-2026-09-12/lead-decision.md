# Final ranking judgment — 12 September 2026

**Intuit and Wolters Kluwer are my first two candidates. Accenture is third on current-price attractiveness, with substantially weaker conviction that AI benefits accrue to its shareholders.** None offers a large valuation cushion under these assumptions. I would not turn this research into a broad basket of presumed AI winners at current prices.

The completed work contains an [operating-fit ranking of all 100 companies](universe-ranking.md), a separate [investment ordering of 25 valued candidates](investment-ranking.md), and **75 five-year scenarios**: downside, base and bull for each valued company. The remaining 75 have evidence reviews and an operating rank, but **no completed current-price valuation in this release**. Their absence from the valued group is not a valuation rejection. The 25 cover leading thesis candidates and decision-relevant comparisons across workflows, platforms, networks, insurance and pharmaceuticals; selection was not based on obtaining positive modeled returns.

The underlying research and bounded challenges in this phase used GPT-6 Astra with extra-high reasoning. The lead owns the ranking, overrides and final interpretation. Neither an agent's favorable opinion nor a source-audit pass automatically becomes an investment pass.

Only three cases strictly exceed the illustrative 10% base hurdle. LSEG rounds to 10.0% but is slightly below it. The 10% threshold is a research convention, not a required return supplied by the user. At 12%, **none of the 25 qualifies**. For a more useful price cushion, the corresponding base entries are about **USD303 for Intuit, EUR63 for WKL and USD173 for Accenture**.

The top three's current prices are only about 2.5–3.6% below their 10% entry thresholds. Reducing their earnings anchors by 10% lowers base IRRs to roughly 8.4–8.8%; cutting terminal multiples by 20% lowers them to roughly 6.0–6.5%. This is a focused shortlist for disciplined underwriting, not evidence of a broad margin of safety. The [sensitivity table](sensitivity.md) exposes the same tests for every candidate.

## What the base and bull cases actually assume

**Intuit:** The base starts with USD20.24 of FY2027 EPS, the midpoint of issuer GAAP guidance, and then applies four further years of 8% per-share growth. At 18 times terminal earnings, year-five price is USD495.65, with USD30.50 of dividends over the holding period. The bull uses 10% subsequent growth, a separately conditioned 10% net terminal AI increment and a 22 times multiple, reaching USD717.13 before dividends. The downside assumes guidance is missed, followed by stagnation and a 13 times multiple; annual IRR is −5.5%. Existing product innovation is already embedded in the guided year-one earnings. [Issuer FY2026 results and FY2027 guidance](https://investors.intuit.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1320/intuit-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-fiscal-2026-results-sets-fiscal-2027-guidance).

**Wolters Kluwer:** The base uses EUR5.20 of normalized annual earnings, including a recurring-cost allowance, 5% per-share growth and a 14 times terminal multiple. Year-five price is EUR92.87, plus EUR14.62 of dividends. The bull uses 7% growth, a separate 5% net AI increment and an 18 times multiple, reaching EUR137.78. The downside includes a 5% annual earnings decline and a 10 times multiple, producing −6.1% annual IRR. Its regulated workflows support the thesis, but renewal economics and recurring cash conversion must survive transition costs. [Normalization, source filings and case](cases/C035.md).

**Accenture:** The base uses the midpoint of FY2026 GAAP EPS guidance, USD13.44, then 4% growth and a 16 times terminal multiple. Year-five price is USD261.63, plus USD34.62 of dividends. The bull needs 7% growth, a small 3% net AI increment and a 19 times multiple; annual IRR is 17.9%. In the downside, pricing and billable-volume pressure cause earnings to shrink, producing −6.4% annual IRR. A low-looking P/E does not resolve who retains consulting productivity gains. [Issuer Q3 FY2026 release](https://investor.accenture.com/~/media/Files/A/accenture-v4/investors/earnings-reports/2026/accenture-3q-fy26-earnings-release.pdf).

All 25 base cases assign **zero additional AI premium**. This does not assume AI disappears: deployed products and existing automation remain in the earnings anchor and ordinary business execution. The separate bull increment must exceed that path and be retained after model costs, human review, customer sharing, compensation and investment. Pharmaceutical cases receive no AI-driven improvement in clinical success probabilities.

Most bull upside comes from conventional growth and valuation, not the separate AI increment. For Intuit, the USD221.47 increase from base to bull terminal value consists of USD37.75 from the stronger ordinary earnings path, USD53.34 from AI valued at the base multiple, and USD130.39 from the higher multiple. For WKL, the analogous contributions are EUR9.19, EUR5.10 and EUR30.62. This sequential arithmetic depends on calculation order; it is not a causal attribution. Incremental AI alone adds about 2.16 points to Intuit's bull IRR and 1.06 points to WKL's. A bull case should therefore not be mistaken for an AI-derived expected return.

## Why attractive businesses fall down the price ranking

RELX, Intuit, WKL, Thomson Reuters and Veeva occupy the first five operating-fit positions. Price changes that ordering materially. Veeva's base return is only 4.2% even with 12% ordinary earnings growth and a 30 times terminal multiple. SAP produces 6.5% despite 10% growth. I would keep both on the thesis watchlist and apply entry discipline.

The platform normalizations are decisive. Removing investment gains produces starting EPS of roughly USD16.63 for Microsoft, USD9.67 for Alphabet and USD6.83 for Amazon; Meta's tax-normalized anchor is about USD27.88. At the dated prices, Alphabet is approximately 35 times normalized earnings and Amazon 38 times. Current cash after capex, lease principal and economic SBC is substantially below earnings for all four. These cash measures are stress screens, not estimates of maintenance owner cash. Their terminal earnings valuations remain conditional on earning adequate returns on current investment. [Annual bridges and primary sources](platform-normalization.md).

Alphabet and Salesforce fail the current-price comparison most clearly: both base IRRs are negative, and their bull returns remain below 8%. These conclusions follow the stated normalization and multiples; they are not claims that the businesses lack valuable franchises. Uber nearly reaches the base hurdle, but its upside cannot be considered independently of AV suppliers, insurance and strategic investment. ServiceNow's forward acquisition perimeter is unresolved, so a low return on historical GAAP-derived earnings should not be presented as a definitive intrinsic value.

For insurers, catastrophe experience and reserve releases require normalization before discussing AI savings. For pharmaceuticals, the cash and pipeline replacement reserves are explicit analyst assumptions; they are not substitutes for product-by-product patent and clinical valuation. These considerations explain why apparent low headline multiples do not automatically create a favorable decision.

## Evidence limits and promotion conditions

The case files contain falsifiers and price thresholds. The next promotion decision should be driven by evidence that can change the conclusion: paid workflow economics after all costs for Intuit/WKL/RELX/TRI; an exact diluted earnings bridge for LSEG; retained pricing economics for Accenture; and cash returns on capital for infrastructure-heavy platforms. Generic deployment announcements are insufficient.

Several concrete corrections are already incorporated. Thomson Reuters' USD150m prospective SBC deduction is now anchored to USD124m of actual TTM expense and USD148m at the latest-quarter annualized pace. Intuit and Salesforce use a guided year-one earnings anchor followed by **four**, not five, further growth years. AstraZeneca uses the displayed USD156.10 after-hours indication after same-day news, with USD160.17 regular-close sensitivity; its exact after-hours trade timestamp is unavailable, so it is excluded from strict executable-price comparisons. [TRI interim report](https://thomsonreuters.gcs-web.com/static-files/842c9fc0-bdf4-4b1b-9f6b-c5a14de0b3cf), [AZN quote](https://www.google.com/finance/quote/AZN:NYSE).

The cases use a five-year holding period and annual dividend cash flows. Returns are in each listing's currency, before investor taxes, fees and FX; GBP and GBX, ordinary shares and ADRs are reconciled in the packets. No scenario probabilities or portfolio weights are assigned. The downside case is neither a maximum drawdown estimate nor a loss floor. A mathematically correct valuation remains only as reliable as its business, normalization and capital assumptions.

The [calculation script](../../scripts/value_ranked_candidates.py) reproduces the 75 scenarios, entry prices, reverse-growth requirements and sensitivities. [Validation](validation.json) checks cash-flow IRR identities, year-one timing, scenario ordering and discount-rate sensitivity. Source and accounting reviews are separate from those arithmetic tests.
