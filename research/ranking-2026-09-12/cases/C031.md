## Incremental AI and valuation discipline

Bull-only 5% terminal net uplift, deliberately smaller than headline existing-analytics claims. Present-scale USD 82 m after-tax increment on USD 1642 m benchmark earnings implies approximatelyUSD 110 m pretax at 25.5% tax. A distinct net 2% revenue addition at 50% incremental contribution margin would supply aboutUSD 84 m pretax; aboutUSD 26 m net internal savings completes it. Revenue contribution is after data/model/servicing costs; released labor is not counted twice. These are analyst requirements, not observed conversion economics.

At the stated quote, the base case returns **8.9%**. The 10% base entry is **26.48 GBP**, versus 28.93 at 8% and 24.28 at 12%. Holding base multiple/dividends fixed requires **9.2%** ordinary earnings growth for 10% investor IRR.

In the bull case, the separate AI increment adds 1.07 percentage points of annual IRR. The remainder comes from the conventional earnings path, starting price, multiple and dividends; the whole bull return cannot be attributed to AI.

Base IRR falls to 6.7% with a 10% lower earnings anchor, 4.4% with a 20% lower terminal multiple, and 7.0% with ordinary growth 2 points lower.

## Limits and what must hold

Refreshed decision data and distribution are durable; mature ML is baseline, so new generative upside needs separate paid or fixed-risk evidence.

- GBP returns vary with USD/GBP; EUR investor also bears GBP/EUR or can analyze underlying USD exposure directly.
- Transaction/restructuring expenses recur and benchmark earnings may overstate owner earnings.
- Exact future diluted shares after ongoing buybacks unknown; no point-in-time market-cap denominator is needed for this EPS model.
- Credit cycle, bureau data quality/remediation and consumer breach-contract run-off can swamp incremental AI benefits.

- Falsifier: Organic B 2 B growth below 4% and benchmark margin fails to improve over two reporting periods.
- Falsifier: Controlled lending experiments show no better quality-adjusted approvals at fixed loss/fairness after data and model costs.
- Falsifier: Growth in agent engagements fails to improve retained marketplace contribution or Patient Access net contract economics.

These local-currency equity returns exclude investor tax, fees and FX. Interest is already in earnings; debt is not deducted again. SBC remains an economic cost. Scenario downside is not a loss floor.
