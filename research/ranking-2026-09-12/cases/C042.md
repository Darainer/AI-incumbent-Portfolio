## Incremental AI and valuation discipline

Bull-only 10% net terminal uplift equals $148 m additional annual earnings at present scale. At an assumed 35% after-tax incremental contribution, this needs $423 m revenue beyond the conventional growth path, after model/service/credit or insurance costs. Net internal cost removal could substitute, but must be separately measured and cannot be counted again as product-margin improvement. No released working capital, customer balances, insurance reserves or acquisition addbacks count as recurring AI earnings.

At the stated quote, the base case returns **1.3%**. The 10% base entry is **87.86 USD**, versus 96.30 at 8% and 80.29 at 12%. Holding base multiple/dividends fixed requires **30.3%** ordinary earnings growth for 10% investor IRR.

In the bull case, the separate AI increment adds 2.14 percentage points of annual IRR. The remainder comes from the conventional earnings path, starting price, multiple and dividends; the whole bull return cannot be attributed to AI.

Base IRR falls to-0.8% with a 10% lower earnings anchor, -3.1% with a 20% lower terminal multiple, and-0.4% with ordinary growth 2 points lower.
