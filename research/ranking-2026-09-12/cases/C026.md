## Incremental AI and valuation discipline

Four-percent terminal earnings requires incremental Visa fees and services, not total fraud avoided. At FY 2025 after-tax earnings scale, a roughlyUSD 800 m annual contribution is material; at 50% after-tax incremental contribution this requires aboutUSD 1.6 bn of additional retained fee revenue. This is an aggressive requirement relative to the earlier small own-cost illustration, hence zero base increment.

At the stated quote, the base case returns **6.4%**. The 10% base entry is **314.62 USD**, versus 344.39 at 8% and 287.92 at 12%. Holding base multiple/dividends fixed requires **12.7%** ordinary earnings growth for 10% investor IRR.

In the bull case, the separate AI increment adds 0.87 percentage points of annual IRR. The remainder comes from the conventional earnings path, starting price, multiple and dividends; the whole bull return cannot be attributed to AI.

Base IRR falls to 4.2% with a 10% lower earnings anchor, 1.9% with a 20% lower terminal multiple, and 4.5% with ordinary growth 2 points lower.

## Limits and what must hold

Network fees and value-added services can retain part of improved legitimate approvals; issuer fraud savings are not Visa earnings.

- Government/interchange litigation and rebates can persist, so not all issuer adjustments are added back.
- Terminal 27 x remains a premium and requires durable low-capital network economics.

These local-currency equity returns exclude investor tax, fees and FX. Interest is already in earnings; debt is not deducted again. SBC remains an economic cost. Scenario downside is not a loss floor.
