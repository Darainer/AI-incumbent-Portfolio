**Downside:** Dupixent erosion/competition around 2031, weaker vaccines and unsuccessful pipeline replenishment offset existing launches; dividend cut assumed; no AI benefit.

**Base:** Existing Dupixent indications and approved launches offset legacy erosion; growth much below 2026 CER guidance because of concentration, 2031 exclusivity risk, financing and replacement R&D. ## Incremental AI and valuation discipline

AI uplift is a capped bull option for net retained operational savings; see scenario rationale. No AI-driven change in clinical success or insurance risk is modeled.

At the stated quote, the base case returns **9.4%**. The 10% base entry is **71.54 EUR**, versus 77.73 at 8% and 65.96 at 12%. Holding base multiple/dividends fixed requires **4.6%** ordinary earnings growth for 10% investor IRR.

In the bull case, the separate AI increment adds 0.62 percentage points of annual IRR. The remainder comes from the conventional earnings path, starting price, multiple and dividends; the whole bull return cannot be attributed to AI.

Base IRR falls to 7.5% with a 10% lower earnings anchor, 5.4% with a 20% lower terminal multiple, and 7.6% with ordinary growth 2 points lower.

## Limits and what must hold

Large proprietary scientific assets could improve selection, but recent pipeline risks preclude using AI to repair the ordinary case.

- Dupixent exact patent-defense outcomes and erosion speed dominate terminal value; 2031 is an explicit risk horizon, not a certainty that all exclusivity disappears then.
- H 1 2026 guidance is CER; current EUR EPS cannot simply compound CER guidance.
- Major acquisition/replacement spending could exceed the 20% reserve; test normalization factors 70%, 80%, 90%.
- No disclosed prospective AI clinical or retained-cash evidence; bull AI option is not a forecast.
- Require exact diluted and period-end outstanding shares for a full transaction-grade equity bridge.

These local-currency equity returns exclude investor tax, fees and FX. Interest is already in earnings; debt is not deducted again. SBC remains an economic cost. Scenario downside is not a loss floor.
