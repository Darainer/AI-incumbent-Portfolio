## Incremental AI and valuation discipline

A 5% AI uplift is a net content/workflow-pricing option. It requires paid machine consumption and efficiency to exceed upstream royalties, Microsoft distribution sharing and displaced desktop spend. Existing company AI/efficiency programmes are baseline, not added again.

At the stated quote, the base case returns **10.0%**. The 10% base entry is **82.32 GBP**, versus 89.97 at 8% and 75.45 at 12%. Holding base multiple/dividends fixed requires **8.0%** ordinary earnings growth for 10% investor IRR.

In the bull case, the separate AI increment adds 1.09 percentage points of annual IRR. The remainder comes from the conventional earnings path, starting price, multiple and dividends; the whole bull return cannot be attributed to AI.

Base IRR falls to 7.8% with a 10% lower earnings anchor, 5.4% with a 20% lower terminal multiple, and 8.0% with ordinary growth 2 points lower.
