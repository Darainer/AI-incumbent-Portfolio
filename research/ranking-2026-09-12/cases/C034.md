## Incremental AI and valuation discipline

Bull-only net terminal uplift 6%: about 4% group earnings from Legal product contribution, requiring roughly one-third incremental Legal earnings at a circa 12% group-profit weight, plus 2% internal net savings. Illustrative present-scale internal bridge: eligible cost GBP 1562 m (25% of FY adjusted cost base) x 15% task efficiency x 60% realization x 50% retention minus GBP 10 m recurring cost, after 22.5% tax, is GBP 46.7 m or about 2% FY adjusted net profit. This is a calibrated analyst upper case, not a disclosed company result; no released labor is also counted in product contribution.

At the stated quote, the base case returns **8.2%**. The 10% base entry is **22.92 GBP**, versus 25.00 at 8% and 21.05 at 12%. Holding base multiple/dividends fixed requires **7.9%** ordinary earnings growth for 10% investor IRR.

In the bull case, the separate AI increment adds 1.24 percentage points of annual IRR. The remainder comes from the conventional earnings path, starting price, multiple and dividends; the whole bull return cannot be attributed to AI.

Base IRR falls to 6.2% with a 10% lower earnings anchor, 3.9% with a 20% lower terminal multiple, and 6.4% with ordinary growth 2 points lower.

## Limits and what must hold

Best combined evidence of paid workflow adoption, maintained content and an existing cash-generating franchise; bundle value shares are not incremental AI uplift.

- Current price is a vendor close, not independently certified exchange auction print.
- Acquired-intangible replacement economics and acquisition spending are not fully captured by adjusted EPS; terminal multiples and lower growth compensate only approximately.
- All AI attribution remains below controlled net-economics evidence; interface ownership, licensing rights and cohort economics unresolved.
- Leverage rose; 2026 accelerated buybacks cannot be perpetually extrapolated.

- Falsifier: Two reporting periods of Legal underlying growth below 5% together with flat/negative seat-adjusted renewal revenue.
- Falsifier: Current-version controlled tests fail to reduce completion plus verification cost against credible rivals.
- Falsifier: Net debt/EBITDA persists above 2.5 x without corresponding organic profit growth.

These local-currency equity returns exclude investor tax, fees and FX. Interest is already in earnings; debt is not deducted again. SBC remains an economic cost. Scenario downside is not a loss floor.
