## Incremental AI and valuation discipline

Bull-only 8% net terminal uplift equals $411 m additional annual earnings at present scale. At an assumed 35% after-tax incremental contribution, this needs $1174 m revenue beyond the conventional growth path, after model/service/credit or insurance costs. Net internal cost removal could substitute, but must be separately measured and cannot be counted again as product-margin improvement. No released working capital, customer balances, insurance reserves or acquisition addbacks count as recurring AI earnings.

At the stated quote, the base case returns **9.7%**. The 10% base entry is **70.65 USD**, versus 77.44 at 8% and 64.56 at 12%. Holding base multiple/dividends fixed requires **15.3%** ordinary earnings growth for 10% investor IRR.

In the bull case, the separate AI increment adds 1.85 percentage points of annual IRR. The remainder comes from the conventional earnings path, starting price, multiple and dividends; the whole bull return cannot be attributed to AI.

Base IRR falls to 7.4% with a 10% lower earnings anchor, 4.9% with a 20% lower terminal multiple, and 7.8% with ordinary growth 2 points lower.
