## Incremental AI and valuation discipline

Bull-only 10% terminal earnings. A present-scale equivalent of aboutUSD 456.6 m after tax requires roughlyUSD 578 m pretax at 21% tax. Illustrative non-overlapping mix:4% incremental GBS revenue on 12.864 bn at 60% net contribution=309 m pretax, plus 269 m net internal savings after all technology/review costs. The internal portion requires approximately 25% task efficiency ×70% realization ×70% retention onUSD 3 bn eligible spend less 99 m recurring costs. These are requirements, not measured outcomes; the total effect is only~1.9% annualized across five years.

At the stated quote, the base case returns **10.6%**. The 10% base entry is **330.67 USD**, versus 361.51 at 8% and 303.00 at 12%. Holding base multiple/dividends fixed requires **7.2%** ordinary earnings growth for 10% investor IRR.

In the bull case, the separate AI increment adds 2.16 percentage points of annual IRR. The remainder comes from the conventional earnings path, starting price, multiple and dividends; the whole bull return cannot be attributed to AI.

Base IRR falls to 8.4% with a 10% lower earnings anchor, 6.0% with a 20% lower terminal multiple, and 9.1% with ordinary growth 2 points lower.

## Limits and what must hold

Ledger and tax execution create a direct route to paid completion; customer value must survive tax-unit weakness, rival agents and human review cost.

- FY 2027 guidance must hold; a 20% year 1 miss reduces the entry threshold approximately 20% before dividend effects.
- Tax volumes, Mailchimp, accountant-channel retention and external-agent pricing are decision-critical.
- Neither cohort revenue uplift nor net internal cost realization has been demonstrated.

These local-currency equity returns exclude investor tax, fees and FX. Interest is already in earnings; debt is not deducted again. SBC remains an economic cost. Scenario downside is not a loss floor.
