## Incremental AI and valuation discipline

Six-percent incremental owner cash requires roughlyUSD 117 m present-scale net contribution after all capital, serving, verification and customer-sharing costs. A 40% after-tax contribution would need~USD 293 m incremental revenue after cannibalization. PriorUSD 200 m+ existing AI investment is already in the guided baseline.

At the stated quote, the base case returns **8.7%**. The 10% base entry is **92.18 USD**, versus 100.59 at 8% and 84.63 at 12%. Holding base multiple/dividends fixed requires **8.3%** ordinary earnings growth for 10% investor IRR.

In the bull case, the separate AI increment adds 1.26 percentage points of annual IRR. The remainder comes from the conventional earnings path, starting price, multiple and dividends; the whole bull return cannot be attributed to AI.

Base IRR falls to 6.6% with a 10% lower earnings anchor, 4.4% with a 20% lower terminal multiple, and 6.9% with ordinary growth 2 points lower.

## Limits and what must hold

Strong legal/tax content and paid workflows; more concentrated legal-interface competition and restricted customer-data reuse temper the advantage.

- SBC reserve, post-quarter financing and disposed-business perimeter remain unresolved; this is explicitly a conditional cash sensitivity.
- Customer prompts/content are not an unrestricted training moat.
- Price change sensitivity is easy; normalization uncertainty is more important than small terminal-multiple changes.
- USD 150 m SBC is a prospective expense assumption, not reported FY 26 actual; use USD 124 m TTM sensitivity separately.
- Guidance realization and post-September 10 financing / Print JV perimeter remain conditions. TTM SBC evidence is resolved, but this is not a fully pro-forma forecast.

These local-currency equity returns exclude investor tax, fees and FX. Interest is already in earnings; debt is not deducted again. SBC remains an economic cost. Scenario downside is not a loss floor.
