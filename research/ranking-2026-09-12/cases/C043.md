## Incremental AI and valuation discipline

A 10% uplift must be incremental to current Agentforce revenue already in guidance. At normalized next-year net income roughlyUSD 6.147 bn, present-scale requirement is 615 m after tax, about 791 m pretax at 23.5% tax; a 40% incremental margin requires~USD 2.01 bn net incremental revenue after cannibalization. ARR scope changes and work-unit counts do not prove that.

At the stated quote, the base case returns **-2.5%**. The 10% base entry is **137.17 USD**, versus 150.06 at 8% and 125.61 at 12%. Holding base multiple/dividends fixed requires **25.9%** ordinary earnings growth for 10% investor IRR.

In the bull case, the separate AI increment adds 1.97 percentage points of annual IRR. The remainder comes from the conventional earnings path, starting price, multiple and dividends; the whole bull return cannot be attributed to AI.

Base IRR falls to-4.5% with a 10% lower earnings anchor, -6.6% with a 20% lower terminal multiple, and-3.9% with ordinary growth 2 points lower.

## Limits and what must hold

A real commercial agent offering, but seat cannibalization, changing ARR definitions and acquisition-funded leverage require conservative earnings.

- Large acquisition programme and leveraged repurchases constrain balance-sheet flexibility.
- Normalized EPS is far below both headline adjustedEPS and GAAP EPS including investment gains.
- Exact acquired amortization and pro-forma integration outcomes require continuing review; the case retains those costs.

These local-currency equity returns exclude investor tax, fees and FX. Interest is already in earnings; debt is not deducted again. SBC remains an economic cost. Scenario downside is not a loss floor.
