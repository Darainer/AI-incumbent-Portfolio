## Incremental AI and valuation discipline

Bull-only 5% terminal earnings uplift requires an additional $6.01 bn annual net earnings at present scale, or $15.03 bn genuinely incremental revenue at an assumed 40% after-tax contribution after model, service, depreciation and compensation costs. This is a scale requirement, not observed economics. Alternatively, measured net internal savings can supply part, but cannot also be counted in product margins. Growth in AI infrastructure sales already inside conventional EPS growth does not qualify again. Without a funded capital-recovery bridge, the bull remains conditional.

At the stated quote, the base case returns **-1.3%**. The 10% base entry is **197.99 USD**, versus 216.86 at 8% and 181.07 at 12%. Holding base multiple/dividends fixed requires **20.4%** ordinary earnings growth for 10% investor IRR.

In the bull case, the separate AI increment adds 1.04 percentage points of annual IRR. The remainder comes from the conventional earnings path, starting price, multiple and dividends; the whole bull return cannot be attributed to AI.

Base IRR falls to-3.3% with a 10% lower earnings anchor, -5.5% with a 20% lower terminal multiple, and-3.1% with ordinary growth 2 points lower.

## Limits and what must hold

Search and cloud distribution are powerful, but interface disruption, compute commitments and common-share dilution make incremental capture uncertain.

- Cash after current investment is substantially below normalized earnings; maintenance versus growth capex cannot be estimated reliably.
- The terminal earnings multiple requires adequate future returns on capex, leased capacity and AI counterparties. This is not a verified owner-FCF valuation.
- Ordinary growth already includes existing AI products, recommendation/ML and announced business execution. Bull AI is only the additional net retention above that path.
- No uncommenced lease or purchase commitment is mechanically added twice to debt.

Lead overrides of agent inputs:

- Zero separate base AI premium; already deployed AI remains in baseline/conventional execution. Recompute dividend-timed IRR, replacing agent terminal-wealth CAGR.
- Stronger downside: ordinary EPS stagnation for MSFT/AMZN and 3% annual decline for GOOGL; agent positive-growth downside did not adequately test failed capital recovery.

These local-currency equity returns exclude investor tax, fees and FX. Interest is already in earnings; debt is not deducted again. SBC remains an economic cost. Scenario downside is not a loss floor.
