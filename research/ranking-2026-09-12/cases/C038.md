## Incremental AI and valuation discipline

Eight-percent terminal contribution is bounded. Present-scale equivalent~USD 81 m net, or~USD 108 m pretax at 25% tax, would need aroundUSD 240 m incremental annual revenue at 45% fully loaded contribution. It must exclude CRM migration already in ordinary growth and include model/validation costs.

At the stated quote, the base case returns **4.2%**. The 10% base entry is **200.20 USD**, versus 219.44 at 8% and 182.95 at 12%. Holding base multiple/dividends fixed requires **18.2%** ordinary earnings growth for 10% investor IRR.

In the bull case, the separate AI increment adds 1.71 percentage points of annual IRR. The remainder comes from the conventional earnings path, starting price, multiple and dividends; the whole bull return cannot be attributed to AI.

Base IRR falls to 2.0% with a 10% lower earnings anchor, -0.3% with a 20% lower terminal multiple, and 2.3% with ordinary growth 2 points lower.

## Limits and what must hold

Validated life-science systems and an actual field-team deployment support a meaningful workflow thesis; AI attach remains separate from CRM migration.

- A high current multiple leaves little room for migration delays.
- A top 20 field-team deployment is real but neither a disclosed paid-AI denominator nor controlled group cash evidence.

These local-currency equity returns exclude investor tax, fees and FX. Interest is already in earnings; debt is not deducted again. SBC remains an economic cost. Scenario downside is not a loss floor.
