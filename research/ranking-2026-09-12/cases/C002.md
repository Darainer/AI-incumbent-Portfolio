## Incremental AI and valuation discipline

AI uplift is a capped bull option for net retained operational savings; see scenario rationale. No AI-driven change in clinical success or insurance risk is modeled.

At the stated quote, the base case returns **1.5%**. The 10% base entry is **235.69 CHF**, versus 256.73 at 8% and 216.78 at 12%. Holding base multiple/dividends fixed requires **13.5%** ordinary earnings growth for 10% investor IRR.

In the bull case, the separate AI increment adds 0.78 percentage points of annual IRR. The remainder comes from the conventional earnings path, starting price, multiple and dividends; the whole bull return cannot be attributed to AI.

Base IRR falls to-0.5% with a 10% lower earnings anchor, -2.5% with a 20% lower terminal multiple, and-0.3% with ordinary growth 2 points lower.
