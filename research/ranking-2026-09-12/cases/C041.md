# SAP — C041

Lead-reviewed case, 12 September 2026. **VALUEABLE_WITH_LIMITATIONS**. Operating-fit rank 10 of 100; investment rank 13 of 25.

**Decision: Strong operating fit; insufficient base return.** SAP has a credible workflow and installed-base position, yet 10% ordinary EPS growth still produces only 6.5% investor IRR at the quote. Cloud migration and ordinary operating leverage must not be relabeled incremental AI. The bull needs both stronger execution and a premium multiple.

**What changes the decision:** About EUR 151.47 supports 10% base IRR. Require paid agent economics net of seat cannibalization, service costs and ongoing compensation.

## Incremental AI and valuation discipline

Bull-only 10% net terminal earnings uplift. Present-scale normalized earningsEUR 7.42 bn implyEUR 742 m net, aroundEUR 1.06 bn pretax at 30% tax. Distinct net 5% group revenue uplift at 45% incremental contribution margin supplies aboutEUR 828 m pretax; roughlyEUR 232 m net internal savings completes the requirement. Contribution margins include model/cloud/service cost and net seat cannibalization; do not also count product-development capacity as removed payroll. This is demanding and conditional, not paidAIARR guidance.

At the stated quote, the base case returns **6.5%**. The 10% base entry is **151.47 EUR**, versus 165.55 at 8% and 138.82 at 12%. Holding base multiple/dividends fixed requires **13.8%** ordinary earnings growth for 10% investor IRR.

In the bull case, the separate AI increment adds 2.09 percentage points of annual IRR. The remainder comes from the conventional earnings path, starting price, multiple and dividends; the whole bull return cannot be attributed to AI.

Base IRR falls to 4.4% with a 10% lower earnings anchor, 2.1% with a 20% lower terminal multiple, and 4.6% with ordinary growth 2 points lower.

## Limits and what must hold

Transaction systems and process context support authorized action; cloud migration, bundled AI and standalone monetization must be separated.

- Audited annual numbers supersede preliminaryQ 4 statement; use only audited-plusH 1 bridge.
- SBC responds to share price and new grants; a rising bull price can increase future compensation cost.
- Recurring restructuring/acquisition costs and capitalized development weaken the equality of adjusted earnings and owner cash.
- Cloud backlog is not incrementalAI revenue; renewals, active workflow execution, inference costs and license-design friction unresolved.
- FY 2026 operating guidance is at constant currencies, while EPS model is nominal reportedEUR.

- Falsifier: Cloud backlog growth falls below 15% while support/licenses continue high-single/double-digit declines.
- Falsifier: Cloud gross margin remains below 72% or acquisition and serving costs eliminate ordinary operating leverage.
- Falsifier: Authorized production workflows and AI renewal contract value fail to become material byFY 2028.
- Falsifier: Repeated large restructuring/litigation adjustments or higher equity-compensation cost prevent reported EPS catching up with adjustedEPS.

These local-currency equity returns exclude investor tax, fees and FX. Interest is already in earnings; debt is not deducted again. SBC remains an economic cost. Scenario downside is not a loss floor.
