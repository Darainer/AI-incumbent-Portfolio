# Full universe — lead operating-regime ranking

12 September 2026 · All 100 reviewed · Current-price valuation is a separate table

This order asks where accumulated incumbent assets most plausibly support incremental, retained AI productivity. It combines the lead review with an [independent Astra challenge](independent-challenge.md). It is a judgmental underwriting order, not a numerical score or a claim that adjacent names differ precisely. Broader bands are more robust than exact positions. The original structural scores were not used to mechanically generate it.

A low regime rank does not mean a poor business or an unattractive stock. Conversely, ServiceNow and Veeva can rank highly here and fail a price test. Pharma, insurance and some platforms are included in the valuation set to test conventional value even where incremental AI evidence is weak. No ranking waives G1/G2 or resolves a documented source gap.
