## Lead acceptance notes

**JPMorgan Chase (C023):** Q2 adjusted income, capital and expense growth verified. The COO explicitly describes reinvesting released capacity in growth. E2 enterprise deployment is credible; headcount savings and incremental earnings cannot be inferred from usage. Annualized quarterly income is only a scale proxy.

**Sanofi (C001):** FY2025 BOI and two preclinical partner milestones support a bounded research packet. The EUR600m programme PV cannot clear a EUR607m annual hurdle; no clinical or cash inference is accepted.

**Roche (C002):** Roche has compute deployment and partnered pathology inputs. External Paige benchmarks do not establish Roche efficacy. CHF152m annual pathology illustration stays separate from CHF450m R&D PV.

**AstraZeneca (C003):** Reported FY2025 OP is USD13.743bn. The issuer reports AI-enabled discovery use; this is scoped E2, with no demonstrated clinical success-rate uplift. The USD687.15m annual hurdle and USD4.22bn ten-year PV equivalent are explicitly different units.

**Novartis (C004):** Company-specific workflow integration supports narrow E2. Neither partnership expansion nor unrelated clinical failures establish AI efficacy. The annual core-profit hurdle is USD1.09445bn, not programme NPV.

Acceptance checks source support, scope, materiality arithmetic and explicit limits. It is neither an audit opinion nor a buy/sell recommendation. Raw capsules preserve the researcher's handoff; lead notes govern unresolved differences.
