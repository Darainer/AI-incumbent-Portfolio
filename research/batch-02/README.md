## Lead acceptance notes

**Visa (C026):** Latest financial and A2A deployment claims checked. Keep the reported detection improvement at E2: protocol, paid denominator and retained contribution are undisclosed. The illustrative own-cost route is only $88m against a $1.20bn scale hurdle; material G2 economics remain open.

**JPMorgan Chase (C023):** Q2 adjusted income, capital and expense growth verified. The COO explicitly describes reinvesting released capacity in growth. E2 enterprise deployment is credible; headcount savings and incremental earnings cannot be inferred from usage. Annualized quarterly income is only a scale proxy. The $0.524bn annual pre-tax expense-benefit illustration is NOT_COMPARABLE with the $3.38bn annual after-tax scale hurdle without an explicit tax conversion; no above/below conclusion is retained.

**Sanofi (C001):** FY2025 BOI and two preclinical partner milestones support a bounded research packet. The EUR600m programme PV cannot clear a EUR607m annual hurdle; no clinical or cash inference is accepted.

**Roche (C002):** Roche has compute deployment and partnered pathology inputs. External Paige benchmarks do not establish Roche efficacy. CHF152m annual pathology illustration stays separate from CHF450m R&D PV.

**AstraZeneca (C003):** Reported FY2025 OP is USD13.743bn. The issuer reports AI-enabled discovery use; this is scoped E2, with no demonstrated clinical success-rate uplift. The USD687.15m annual hurdle and USD4.22bn ten-year PV equivalent are explicitly different units.

**Linde (C072):** Process-control and maintenance mechanisms are plausible; a reproducible plant-level AI deployment was not established in the audit. Exclude customer energy savings from Linde’s own cost pool and retain a conservative evidence stage.

**Deere (C062):** The operating business and earnings guidance provide context. The inaccessible herbicide-saving claim is not accepted as verified AI evidence; customer savings and Deere’s after-tax guidance screen require separate capture and tax treatment. The $37.5m annual pre-tax operating-benefit illustration is NOT_COMPARABLE with the $243.75m annual after-tax guidance hurdle without an explicit tax conversion; no above/below conclusion is retained.

**Amazon (C053):** Bedrock and AWS AI demand are supported as external adoption. Neither customer counts nor AI revenue run rate establish Amazon internal productivity. The quarterly operating screen is explicitly incomparable with an annual cost illustration.

**Novartis (C004):** Company-specific workflow integration supports narrow E2. Neither partnership expansion nor unrelated clinical failures establish AI efficacy. The annual core-profit hurdle is USD1.09445bn, not programme NPV.

**Novo Nordisk (C005):** Valo remains preclinical programme scope; AWS supports nonregulated internal use, not clinical-report generation. Pipeline failures are not attributed to AI. FY2025 DKK6.383bn annual hurdle is a scale requirement with no clinical economics bridge.

Acceptance checks source support, scope, materiality arithmetic and explicit limits. It is neither an audit opinion nor a buy/sell recommendation. Raw capsules preserve the researcher's handoff; lead notes govern unresolved differences.
