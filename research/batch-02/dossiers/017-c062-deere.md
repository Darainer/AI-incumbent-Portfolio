## Provisional conclusion and conventional business

Deere has a credible incumbent-AI mechanism because it controls installed connected machines, dealer service history and agronomic Operations Center context. The scoped evidence assessment is **E1 capability; reported fleet outcome was not successfully opened for decisive use**. It does **not** establish controlled quality-adjusted improvement across a representative workload or incremental cash retained by shareholders. The investment question therefore remains operational: can management disclose a stable baseline, eligible workload, quality guardrail, realized economics and recurring technology cost?

The latest conventional anchor is [Q3 FY2026 (ended 2 August 2026)](https://www.deere.com/en/our-company/news-and-announcements/news-releases/2026/corporate/deere-reports-third-quarter-net-income-of-1-379-billion/): **$12.608bn net sales and revenues** and **$1.379bn net income; equipment operations margin 14.4%**. These figures are reported unless explicitly labelled adjusted. FY2025 operating cash flow and financing receivables remain the relevant cycle baseline; current-quarter FCF was not isolated in the opened release. Manufacturing debt must be separated from John Deere Financial borrowings; the finance arm funds dealer/customer credit. The principal confounders are cycle, mix, pricing, acquisitions/restructuring, working capital and capital intensity. Existing prediction, optimization and automation are already in these reported results; only incremental improvement from here belongs in an AI bridge.

## AI mechanisms and competitive challenge

**Mechanism 1 — scoped workflow improvement.** See & Spray computer vision identifies weeds and controls individual nozzles; customers reported nearly 50% lower non-residual herbicide use in 2025, but crop, field and chemistry mix are uncontrolled.

**Mechanism 2 — execution or adjacent demand.** autonomy and predictive service can convert machine/field telemetry into fewer operator hours and less downtime; availability is model-, crop- and geography-specific.

**Challenger and substitute.** CNH Industrial/AGCO plus retrofit autonomy and vision suppliers can pair commodity models with farmer data.

**Adverse evidence.** Large-ag demand, crop economics and tariffs dominate earnings; Deere guided to a roughly $1.2bn FY2026 pre-tax tariff burden, so technology adoption cannot be inferred from consolidated margins.

## Measurement contract and materiality arithmetic

The framework’s research convention is **$250m, approximately 5% of the midpoint of FY2026 net-income guidance ($4.75–5.0bn)**. This is a scale test, not a forecast or valuation.

| KPI / unit | Baseline and comparator | Eligible workload | Quality condition | Known result / next proof event |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Herbicide gallons/acre | same field/chemistry broadcast pass | See & Spray acres | same weed control, yield and re-spray | 2025 portfolio average reported; matched field study |
| Unattended acres/operator-hour | same model with operator | autonomy-ready acres | same safety, downtime and agronomy | machine cohort and incident audit |

Illustrative cost bridge: `$3.0bn eligible annual service, warranty, engineering and support cost × 15% task efficiency × 50% realization × 50% retained share − $75m recurring AI expense = $37.5m annual operating benefit`. All bridge inputs are assumptions. The result is below the hurdle. It excludes customer savings; any revenue case must use incremental company contribution after cannibalization, servicing and model cost. Capex and working capital remain separate.

## Hypotheses, falsifiers and evidence gates

**Strongest positive hypothesis:** the installed asset and distribution let Deere embed models in a difficult workflow, observe outcomes and retain part of better throughput, decisions or service. **Strongest negative hypothesis:** generic models and rivals reach the same customer context, contractual rights block pooled learning, and customers, workers or suppliers capture the gain while implementation/capital cost remains with the company.

Three falsifiers: (1) by the next two annual reporting cycles, management still cannot disclose active production penetration and a quality-adjusted KPI for a material workload; (2) renewals, usage or contribution margin deteriorate as AI pricing, cannibalization and supplier costs rise; (3) matched rollouts fail to beat the existing production system after errors, rework, safety/risk and implementation cost. Core-business deterioration must be diagnosed separately from AI failure.

Unresolved questions are the precise customer-data licenses; whether feedback may improve a pooled model; eligible workload; representative baseline; error and escalation rate; incremental price/volume; labor realization; recurring inference/support cost; required capex; and the share of savings retained after competition or regulation.

**G0:** provisionally satisfied: asset, mechanisms, capture hypothesis and falsifiers are explicit. **G1:** provisionally satisfied only subject to the stated balance-sheet, governance and rights gaps. **G2:** provisional **watch / incomplete**: E1 capability; reported fleet outcome was not successfully opened for decisive use; E3 quality-adjusted evidence and E4 retained cash are absent. **G3/G4:** deferred by user. Confidence is **moderate** in mechanism, **low-to-moderate** in broad deployment and **low** in capture.
