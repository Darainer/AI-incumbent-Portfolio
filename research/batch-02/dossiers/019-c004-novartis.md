# C004 — Novartis evidence dossier

**Unreviewed agent work.** Issuer: Novartis AG; ordinary registered shares NOVN on SIX Swiss Exchange (CHF), with NYSE ADR NVS; reporting currency USD. Research cutoff and retrieval date: 12 September 2026. Latest financial period reviewed: six months ended 30 June 2026, published 21 July 2026; FY2025 is the latest audited year.

## Provisional conclusion and conventional business

Novartis is a focused innovative-medicines company after the 2023 Sandoz separation. Its valuable conventional assets are validated targets and compounds, experimental histories, regulatory and clinical-development capabilities, manufacturing—particularly radioligand therapy—and commercial access. AI can improve molecule and trial decisions, but the public evidence establishes tools and production use, not a higher clinical success rate or retained cash. The September 2026 failures of pelacarsen and acquired del-desiran are unusually useful adverse evidence: computational acceleration cannot substitute for prospective clinical validation.

FY2025 net sales were **$54.532bn**, reported operating income **$17.644bn**, core operating income **$21.889bn** (40.1% margin), operating cash flow **$19.144bn**, and company-defined free cash flow **$17.596bn**. Net debt ended at **$21.9bn**, up from $16.1bn because free cash flow was outweighed by $9.2bn treasury-share cash outflow, $7.8bn dividends and $5.2bn net acquisition/intangible outflow. These figures and reconciliations are in the [FY2025 release](https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-delivered-high-single-digit-sales-growth-achieved-40-core-margin-and-further-advanced-pipeline-2025); core results and FCF are non-IFRS. The 2026 opening outlook was low-single-digit sales growth and low-single-digit core-operating-income decline at constant currency, reflecting major generic erosion. The [Q2 2026 results landing page](https://www.novartis.com/) confirms that H1 results were issued before cutoff; the detailed report was not reliably machine-readable in this research environment, so no unaudited H1 numbers are asserted.

Economics remain drug-specific: patents and regulatory exclusivity support high gross margins, while loss of exclusivity creates abrupt erosion; pipeline failures and acquisitions can swamp modest productivity. Growth brands including Kisqali, Kesimpta, Pluvicto, Scemblix and Cosentyx drove 2025 growth, but Entresto/Promacta generic effects reduced Q4 growth. Capital allocation includes dividends, buybacks and pipeline M&A. The 2025 balance sheet could fund research, but higher net debt and acquisition execution are material constraints.

## AI mechanisms, competitors and evidence

1. **Discovery decision support (ML/generative science).** Experimental history plus protein/chemistry models → rank targets and molecules → assays of fewer, better candidates → potentially more stage-adjusted output per R&D dollar. A Microsoft account says molecule-design support was integrated into medicinal chemists' decision system by 2021. The Isomorphic collaboration began with three targets and expanded in February 2025 by up to three more programs. This is E2 production workflow evidence, but no molecule has disclosed clinical success or incremental economics.
2. **Trial operations (ML/generative assistance).** Protocol and site histories → site selection, recruitment and documentation → shorter cycle time at maintained enrollment quality. Reuters reported in January 2026 that Novartis reduced a site-selection task from weeks to hours. This is a management-reported process result, E2 at most: no matched cohort, total trial duration, cost or cash reconciliation was disclosed.
3. **Enterprise knowledge retrieval (generative assistance).** Governed internal documents → faster retrieval and drafting → released scientific/administrative capacity. Microsoft documented broad employee tooling, but workload penetration, error rates, removed cost and model expense remain unavailable.

The incumbent challenger is **Eli Lilly**. Lilly's [TuneLab release](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-launches-tunelab-platform-give-biotechnology-companies) describes models trained on disposition, safety and preclinical data from hundreds of thousands of molecules, federated learning and partner data contributions. The substitute is an AI-native collaborator such as Isomorphic Labs itself: it can supply multiple pharma companies, limiting Novartis exclusivity and capturing economics via upfront, research and milestone payments. Generic cloud/model tools can also commoditize document work.

## KPI contract and materiality

| KPI | Baseline/comparator | Eligible workload and quality | Known result | Closing event |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| Assay cycles and cost to nominated candidate | Same target class using prior workflow | Named discovery programs; potency/selectivity/toxicity gates | Deployment only | Isomorphic milestone/candidate disclosure |
| Site-selection elapsed hours and trial startup time | Matched historical trials | Trials using tool; enrollment diversity and protocol deviations | Task time claim only | Audited operations study or trial disclosure |

The 5% convention equals **$1.094bn of annual pre-tax operating profit** (0.05 × FY2025 core operating income of $21.889bn). Public disclosures do not support a valid monetary bridge: a program NPV cannot be compared directly with one year's profit. The break-even requirement, stated on a consistent annual pre-tax basis, is `annual stage-adjusted contribution from AI-attributable programs + annual realized operating savings − annual platform/validation expense ≥ $1.094bn`. A future cohort model must discount program cash flows to nomination, attribute only the difference versus matched non-AI cohorts, annualize that difference consistently, and deduct partner economics and full validation cost. None of the needed transition-rate, attribution, cash-flow timing or cost inputs is disclosed. Candidate counts and R&D percentage savings therefore are not substituted.

## Hypotheses, gates and falsifiers

Strong positive: proprietary experimental feedback plus scaled validation lets Novartis eliminate weak programs earlier and nominate better ones, with patent economics retaining value. Strong negative: broadly available models improve every sponsor while biology remains the bottleneck; suppliers and partners capture the benefit, and faster generation increases downstream spending without improving approvals.

Falsifiers: (1) five-year AI-supported cohorts fail to improve stage-adjusted transition or early termination versus matched cohorts; (2) partner programs repeatedly stop before clinical nomination or require steadily richer supplier terms; (3) R&D output per dollar deteriorates after controlling for modality and acquisitions. Mechanism confidence **medium**; deployment **medium**; capture **low**. G0 passes. G1 is provisional because business resilience is verified but acquisition/patent risks remain. G2 is **provisional/insufficient**: scoped E2 exists, but material clinical productivity and retained economics do not. G3/G4 are **deferred by user**. The original 92 structural prior remains a prior; no new numeric score is assigned.

Unresolved: Which pipeline assets used each tool? Who owns model improvements and generated IP? What share of R&D is eligible? Are negative experiments consistently machine-readable? What are compute, validation and partner costs? When will matched cohort data be disclosed?
