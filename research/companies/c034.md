# RELX: initial diligence brief

**ID:** C034 · **Area:** [Legal, tax and professional information](../../industries/i01-legal-tax-and-professional-information.md) · **Evidence:** E2 · **Status:** research only

## Thesis to test

Enriched professional content and decision workflows. AI can convert reference content into validated research, drafting and compliance work.

## Valuation debate

Does a publisher-style valuation underweight paid decision workflows? This is a question to investigate, not a claim about the current share price. Price, consensus, valuation and expected return have not been assessed.

## Observed evidence

- [RELX 2025 annual report: market segments](https://www.relx.com/~/media/Files/R/RELX-Group/documents/reports/annual-reports/2025-ar-sections/relx-2025-market-segments.pdf): Printed page 27 describes Protégé, authoritative content, citation validation and a multi-model approach. Product and adoption claims are management-reported. They do not isolate incremental AI profit.

## Decisive evidence request

AI revenue per legal customer after seat and bundle changes. Require production scope, baseline, comparison group and a bridge to cash earnings. Compare the same task against Thomson Reuters and specialist legal agents. Quantify whether the incumbent's unique assets improve the result after accounting for model access and total cost.

## Principal risk and disconfirmation

Generic agents may weaken seats while trusted content retains value. Area-level falsifier: Generic agents obtain equivalent trusted answers more cheaply, or seat cannibalization exceeds AI monetization.

## Provisional assessment

Structural prior: **95/100**, entirely a hypothesis rather than a validated result. Asset 5/5, AI leverage 5/5, feedback 4/5, testability 4/5, distribution 5/5, capture 5/5. See [dimension rationales](../../universe/score-rationales.csv).

Management execution, data usability and financial resilience require separate evidence. Current downside valuation and AI contribution remain unknown. Confidence is low in a purchase decision until these gaps close.

## Next research packet

1. Latest annual report and results, segment economics, capital allocation and relevant material developments.
2. Data rights, independent customer evidence and competitor task comparison.
3. KPI history and matched baseline, including failed deployments.
4. Conventional-business valuation with zero incremental AI benefit, followed by explicit AI scenarios and a reverse valuation.
