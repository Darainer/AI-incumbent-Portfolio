# SAP: initial diligence brief

**ID:** C041 · **Area:** [Enterprise systems and workflow orchestration](../../industries/i03-enterprise-systems-and-workflow-orchestration.md) · **Evidence:** E2 · **Status:** research only

## Thesis to test

Enterprise processes, permissions and systems of record. AI agents can coordinate transactions across enterprise workflows with existing authorization and audit controls.

## Valuation debate

Does system-of-record control retain value when agents own the interface? This is a question to investigate, not a claim about the current share price. Price, consensus, valuation and expected return have not been assessed.

## Observed evidence

- [SAP Joule and Business AI](https://www.sap.com/products/artificial-intelligence.html): Describes agents operating with business context, integrations and tenant-level data isolation. Customer data access is not equivalent to ownership or cross-customer training permission.

## Decisive evidence request

Incremental gross profit from completed agent workflows. Require production scope, baseline, comparison group and a bridge to cash earnings. Compare the same task against Oracle and independent agent orchestration. Quantify whether the incumbent's unique assets improve the result after accounting for model access and total cost.

## Principal risk and disconfirmation

Third-party agents could capture the user relationship. Area-level falsifier: A cross-vendor agent captures the interface and forces the incumbent into commodity storage pricing.

## Provisional assessment

Structural prior: **91/100**, entirely a hypothesis rather than a validated result. Asset 5/5, AI leverage 4/5, feedback 4/5, testability 4/5, distribution 5/5, capture 5/5. See [dimension rationales](../../universe/score-rationales.csv).

Management execution, data usability and financial resilience require separate evidence. Current downside valuation and AI contribution remain unknown. Confidence is low in a purchase decision until these gaps close.

## Next research packet

1. Latest annual report and results, segment economics, capital allocation and relevant material developments.
2. Data rights, independent customer evidence and competitor task comparison.
3. KPI history and matched baseline, including failed deployments.
4. Conventional-business valuation with zero incremental AI benefit, followed by explicit AI scenarios and a reverse valuation.
