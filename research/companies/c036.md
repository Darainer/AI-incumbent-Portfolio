# Thomson Reuters: initial diligence brief

**ID:** C036 · **Area:** [Legal, tax and professional information](../../industries/i01-legal-tax-and-professional-information.md) · **Evidence:** E1 · **Status:** research only

## Thesis to test

Legal and tax content with verification and workflow tools. AI can convert reference content into validated research, drafting and compliance work.

## Valuation debate

Does content scarcity translate into revenue despite fewer research seats? This is a question to investigate, not a claim about the current share price. Price, consensus, valuation and expected return have not been assessed.

## Observed evidence

- [Thomson Reuters AI for professionals](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en/artificial-intelligence): CoCounsel combines professional content with workflow tools. FAQ says customer content and prompts are not used to train or improve its AI products or underlying models. Commercial description and selected customer examples. Do not assume pooled customer-data training rights.

## Decisive evidence request

Customer-level bill and retention after CoCounsel deployment. Require production scope, baseline, comparison group and a bridge to cash earnings. Compare the same task against RELX and legal AI entrants. Quantify whether the incumbent's unique assets improve the result after accounting for model access and total cost.

## Principal risk and disconfirmation

Customer content is not available for a pooled training flywheel. Area-level falsifier: Generic agents obtain equivalent trusted answers more cheaply, or seat cannibalization exceeds AI monetization.

## Provisional assessment

Structural prior: **91/100**, entirely a hypothesis rather than a validated result. Asset 5/5, AI leverage 4/5, feedback 4/5, testability 4/5, distribution 5/5, capture 5/5. See [dimension rationales](../../universe/score-rationales.csv).

Management execution, data usability and financial resilience require separate evidence. Current downside valuation and AI contribution remain unknown. Confidence is low in a purchase decision until these gaps close.

1. Latest annual report and results, segment economics, capital allocation and relevant material developments.
2. Data rights, independent customer evidence and competitor task comparison.
3. KPI history and matched baseline, including failed deployments.
4. Conventional-business valuation with zero incremental AI benefit, followed by explicit AI scenarios and a reverse valuation.
