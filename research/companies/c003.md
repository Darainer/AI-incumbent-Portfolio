# AstraZeneca: initial diligence brief

**ID:** C003 · **Area:** [Pharmaceuticals and biotechnology](../../industries/h01-pharmaceuticals-and-biotechnology.md) · **Evidence:** E0 · **Status:** research only

## Thesis to test

Oncology and broader clinical-development engine. Use multimodal models and active learning to improve target selection, molecule design and trial stratification. The valuable outcome is more clinically useful candidates per research euro.

## Valuation debate

Does pipeline strength leave any unpriced productivity upside? This is a question to investigate, not a claim about the current share price. Price, consensus, valuation and expected return have not been assessed.

## Observed evidence

No company-specific source has yet been reviewed. The asset and advantage statements below are research hypotheses. Obtain the latest annual report, relevant results and a direct product or deployment source before changing this status.

## Decisive evidence request

Clinical productivity after controlling for oncology mix and acquisitions. Require production scope, baseline, comparison group and a bridge to cash earnings. Compare the same task against Roche, Sanofi and therapeutic-area peers. Quantify whether the incumbent's unique assets improve the result after accounting for model access and total cost.

## Principal risk and disconfirmation

Pipeline breadth can mask correlated clinical and commercial risks. Area-level falsifier: More preclinical candidates without improved prospective clinical translation, or costs move into expensive validation.
