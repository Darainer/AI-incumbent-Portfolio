# Sanofi: initial diligence brief

**ID:** C001 · **Area:** [Pharmaceuticals and biotechnology](../../industries/h01-pharmaceuticals-and-biotechnology.md) · **Evidence:** E1 · **Status:** research only

## Thesis to test

Immunology and vaccine research, clinical cohorts and trial infrastructure. Use multimodal models and active learning to improve target selection, molecule design and trial stratification. The valuable outcome is more clinically useful candidates per research euro.

## Valuation debate

Does a traditional pharma valuation give little credit to a more productive R&D engine? This is a question to investigate, not a claim about the current share price. Price, consensus, valuation and expected return have not been assessed.

## Observed evidence

- [Sanofi artificial intelligence in R&D](https://www.sanofi.com/en/our-science/digital-artificial-intelligence/artificial-intelligence-rd): Describes target identification, active learning for molecule design and integration of clinical and molecular data. Dated deployment description. Does not demonstrate improved Phase II or Phase III success.

## Decisive evidence request

Prospective clinical cohort success and stage-adjusted R&D output. Require production scope, baseline, comparison group and a bridge to cash earnings. Compare the same task against Roche, AstraZeneca and AI-native discovery platforms. Quantify whether the incumbent's unique assets improve the result after accounting for model access and total cost.

## Principal risk and disconfirmation

R&D productivity must translate into clinical successes beyond existing leading franchises. Area-level falsifier: More preclinical candidates without improved prospective clinical translation, or costs move into expensive validation.

## Provisional assessment

Structural prior: **92/100**, entirely a hypothesis rather than a validated result. Asset 5/5, AI leverage 4/5, feedback 5/5, testability 3/5, distribution 5/5, capture 5/5. See [dimension rationales](../../universe/score-rationales.csv).

Management execution, data usability and financial resilience require separate evidence. Current downside valuation and AI contribution remain unknown. Confidence is low in a purchase decision until these gaps close.

## Next research packet

1. Latest annual report and results, segment economics, capital allocation and relevant material developments.
2. Data rights, independent customer evidence and competitor task comparison.
3. KPI history and matched baseline, including failed deployments.
4. Conventional-business valuation with zero incremental AI benefit, followed by explicit AI scenarios and a reverse valuation.
