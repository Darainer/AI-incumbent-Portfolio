# Chubb: initial diligence brief

**ID:** C018 · **Area:** [Property insurance and reinsurance](../../industries/f01-property-insurance-and-reinsurance.md) · **Evidence:** E0 · **Status:** research only

## Thesis to test

Commercial insurance underwriting and claims expertise. AI can interpret submissions, find fraud and improve risk selection. Reinsurance has sparse tail labels, unlike frequent retail claims.

## Valuation debate

Can commercial underwriting expertise keep AI gains through renewals? This is a question to investigate, not a claim about the current share price. Price, consensus, valuation and expected return have not been assessed.

## Observed evidence

No company-specific source has yet been reviewed. The asset and advantage statements below are research hypotheses. Obtain the latest annual report, relevant results and a direct product or deployment source before changing this status.

## Decisive evidence request

Cohort loss and expense ratios at equal risk mix. Require production scope, baseline, comparison group and a bridge to cash earnings. Compare the same task against Other commercial carriers using common tools. Quantify whether the incumbent's unique assets improve the result after accounting for model access and total cost.

## Principal risk and disconfirmation

Hard-market pricing must be separated from AI risk selection. Area-level falsifier: Apparent gains come from reserve releases, a hard pricing cycle, risk exclusion or hidden correlated exposures.
