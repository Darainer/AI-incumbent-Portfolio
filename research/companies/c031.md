# Experian: initial diligence brief

**ID:** C031 · **Area:** [Credit data, exchanges and financial intelligence](../../industries/f05-credit-data-exchanges-and-financial-intelligence.md) · **Evidence:** E0 · **Status:** research only

## Thesis to test

Consumer and business credit files and decision workflows. AI can turn curated records into underwriting, research and compliance decisions with less manual reconciliation.

## Valuation debate

Does the price reward data ownership without reflecting better decision services? This is a question to investigate, not a claim about the current share price. Price, consensus, valuation and expected return have not been assessed.

## Observed evidence

No company-specific source has yet been reviewed. The asset and advantage statements below are research hypotheses. Obtain the latest annual report, relevant results and a direct product or deployment source before changing this status.

## Decisive evidence request

Marginal lift of proprietary attributes over lawful alternative data. Require production scope, baseline, comparison group and a bridge to cash earnings. Compare the same task against Other credit bureaus and lender-built models. Quantify whether the incumbent's unique assets improve the result after accounting for model access and total cost.

## Principal risk and disconfirmation

Consent, fairness and data portability constrain exclusivity. Area-level falsifier: Customers recreate the product with alternative data, or suppliers capture the AI revenue.

## Provisional assessment

Structural prior: **94/100**, entirely a hypothesis rather than a validated result. Asset 5/5, AI leverage 5/5, feedback 5/5, testability 4/5, distribution 5/5, capture 4/5. See [dimension rationales](../../universe/score-rationales.csv).

Management execution, data usability and financial resilience require separate evidence. Current downside valuation and AI contribution remain unknown. Confidence is low in a purchase decision until these gaps close.
