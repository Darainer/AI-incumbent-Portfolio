# Deere: initial diligence brief

**ID:** C062 · **Area:** [Agricultural, construction and mining machinery](../../industries/d02-agricultural-construction-and-mining-machinery.md) · **Evidence:** E0 · **Status:** research only

## Thesis to test

Agricultural machines, dealer distribution and field workflows. Computer vision and autonomy can reduce inputs, operator burden and idle time.

## Valuation debate

Will farmers repeatedly pay for measured input and labor savings? This is a question to investigate, not a claim about the current share price. Price, consensus, valuation and expected return have not been assessed.

## Observed evidence

No company-specific source has yet been reviewed. The asset and advantage statements below are research hypotheses. Obtain the latest annual report, relevant results and a direct product or deployment source before changing this status.

## Decisive evidence request

Renewal, customer payback and outcomes over full seasons. Require production scope, baseline, comparison group and a bridge to cash earnings. Compare the same task against CNH, retrofit vendors and open farm software. Quantify whether the incumbent's unique assets improve the result after accounting for model access and total cost.

## Principal risk and disconfirmation

Farm economics, interoperability and paid adoption. Area-level falsifier: Customer savings do not support durable pricing or autonomy needs expensive supervision.

## Provisional assessment

Structural prior: **91/100**, entirely a hypothesis rather than a validated result. Asset 5/5, AI leverage 5/5, feedback 4/5, testability 4/5, distribution 5/5, capture 4/5. See [dimension rationales](../../universe/score-rationales.csv).

Management execution, data usability and financial resilience require separate evidence. Current downside valuation and AI contribution remain unknown. Confidence is low in a purchase decision until these gaps close.
