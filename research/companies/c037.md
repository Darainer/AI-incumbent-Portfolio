# Intuit: initial diligence brief

**ID:** C037 · **Area:** [Vertical software and financial administration](../../industries/i02-vertical-software-and-financial-administration.md) · **Evidence:** E1 · **Status:** research only

## Thesis to test

Accounting, tax and small-business transaction workflows. AI can perform more bookkeeping, payroll, compliance or industry-specific administration inside the existing workflow.

## Valuation debate

Can automated bookkeeping expand customer value faster than it erodes software pricing? This is a question to investigate, not a claim about the current share price. Price, consensus, valuation and expected return have not been assessed.

## Observed evidence

- [Intuit Assist](https://www.intuit.com/intuitassist/): Describes a generative AI financial assistant integrated with Intuit products. No independently verified contribution margin or causal retention uplift established here.

## Decisive evidence request

Net customer spend and verified completed work after support costs. Require production scope, baseline, comparison group and a bridge to cash earnings. Compare the same task against Xero, accountants using generic agents and tax alternatives. Quantify whether the incumbent's unique assets improve the result after accounting for model access and total cost.

## Principal risk and disconfirmation

Seat substitution, competition and support costs of autonomous work. Area-level falsifier: Cheaper agents use the system only as a low-price database or customers eliminate more seats than they buy AI services.

## Provisional assessment

Structural prior: **91/100**, entirely a hypothesis rather than a validated result. Asset 5/5, AI leverage 5/5, feedback 4/5, testability 4/5, distribution 5/5, capture 4/5. See [dimension rationales](../../universe/score-rationales.csv).

Management execution, data usability and financial resilience require separate evidence. Current downside valuation and AI contribution remain unknown. Confidence is low in a purchase decision until these gaps close.

## Next research packet

1. Latest annual report and results, segment economics, capital allocation and relevant material developments.
2. Data rights, independent customer evidence and competitor task comparison.
3. KPI history and matched baseline, including failed deployments.
4. Conventional-business valuation with zero incremental AI benefit, followed by explicit AI scenarios and a reverse valuation.
