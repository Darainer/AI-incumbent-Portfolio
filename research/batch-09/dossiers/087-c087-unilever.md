## Provisional conclusion and conventional business

Unilever has a credible incumbent-AI mechanism because it controls brands, retailer/channel data, consumer research, formulations and global supply-chain execution. The scoped evidence assessment is **E1 supply-chain capability; no opened controlled cohort**. It does **not** establish controlled quality-adjusted improvement across a representative workload or incremental cash retained by shareholders. The investment question therefore remains operational: can management disclose a stable baseline, eligible workload, quality guardrail, realized economics and recurring technology cost?

The latest conventional anchor is [H1 2026 (ended 30 June 2026)](https://www.unilever.com/investors/results-presentations-webcasts/): **€25.6bn turnover; underlying sales growth 4.8%** and **€5.2bn underlying operating profit, up 0.9%**. These figures are reported unless explicitly labelled adjusted. cash conversion must reflect inventories, restructuring, disposals and brand investment; turnover excludes the separated ice-cream perimeter. net debt, pensions and lease commitments remain meaningful; portfolio transactions complicate comparison. The principal confounders are cycle, mix, pricing, acquisitions/restructuring, working capital and capital intensity. Existing prediction, optimization and automation are already in these reported results; only incremental improvement from here belongs in an AI bridge.

## AI mechanisms and competitive challenge

**Mechanism 1 — scoped workflow improvement.** AI-assisted creative production and media optimization can increase tested content throughput; brand lift and incremental sales after media cost are the endpoint.

**Mechanism 2 — execution or adjacent demand.** forecasting and supply-chain control can reduce waste/service failures; much is established analytics and current restructuring is a major confounder.

**Challenger and substitute.** P&G and L’Oréal have comparable data/brands; retailers, private labels and agencies/model platforms can capture savings.

**Adverse evidence.** commodity/input inflation and pricing response dominate margins; synthetic creative can commoditize execution and harm brand trust without controlled outcomes.

## Measurement contract and materiality arithmetic

The framework’s research convention is **€520m, 5% of a €10.4bn annualized H1 underlying operating-profit proxy**. This is a scale test, not a forecast or valuation.

| KPI / unit | Baseline and comparator | Eligible workload | Quality condition | Known result / next proof event |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Forecast error and waste/SKU | current planning stack | AI-planned SKUs/sites | same service and inventory | matched plant/market cohort |
| Incremental contribution/creative | current agency campaign | AI-produced creatives | same brand safety and media spend | randomized campaign lift after cost |

Illustrative cost bridge: `€12bn eligible supply-chain, marketing-production and support cost × 10% task efficiency × 40% realization × 50% retained share − €150m recurring AI expense = €90m annual operating benefit`. All bridge inputs are assumptions. The result is below the hurdle. It excludes customer savings; any revenue case must use incremental company contribution after cannibalization, servicing and model cost. Capex and working capital remain separate.

## Hypotheses, falsifiers and evidence gates

**Strongest positive hypothesis:** the installed asset and distribution let Unilever embed models in a difficult workflow, observe outcomes and retain part of better throughput, decisions or service. **Strongest negative hypothesis:** generic models and rivals reach the same customer context, contractual rights block pooled learning, and customers, workers or suppliers capture the gain while implementation/capital cost remains with the company.

Three falsifiers: (1) by the next two annual reporting cycles, management still cannot disclose active production penetration and a quality-adjusted KPI for a material workload; (2) renewals, usage or contribution margin deteriorate as AI pricing, cannibalization and supplier costs rise; (3) matched rollouts fail to beat the existing production system after errors, rework, safety/risk and implementation cost. Core-business deterioration must be diagnosed separately from AI failure.

Unresolved questions are the precise customer-data licenses; whether feedback may improve a pooled model; eligible workload; representative baseline; error and escalation rate; incremental price/volume; labor realization; recurring inference/support cost; required capex; and the share of savings retained after competition or regulation.

**G0:** provisionally satisfied: asset, mechanisms, capture hypothesis and falsifiers are explicit. **G1:** provisionally satisfied only subject to the stated balance-sheet, governance and rights gaps. **G2:** provisional **watch / incomplete**: E1 supply-chain capability; no opened controlled cohort; E3 quality-adjusted evidence and E4 retained cash are absent. **G3/G4:** deferred by user. Confidence is **moderate** in mechanism, **low-to-moderate** in broad deployment and **low** in capture.
