## AI mechanism, deployment and rights

The scoped mechanism is: governed operational data → model or retrieval layer → recommendation or bounded automation → human/system action → measurable revenue retention, throughput or avoided cost. For Amadeus IT Group, the relevant deployed surface is **airline retailing and operations**. machine learning improves disruption handling, retailing and operational decisions. Company product material ([AI/product source](https://amadeus.com/en/insights/artificial-intelligence); [workflow source](https://amadeus.com/en/portfolio/airlines)) establishes availability and intended function, but it does not provide a controlled counterfactual.

Rights are narrower than access. Customer records, licensed content, personal data and partner inputs remain subject to contract, consent, purpose limitation, security and retention rules. The company may infer on those inputs to deliver a service without owning them or being free to train a reusable cross-customer model. Outputs may also contain customer confidential information or third-party IP. A defensible evidence package therefore needs a rights map by dataset: owner, permitted purpose, geography, retention, model-improvement permission, deletion obligation and audit right. Public materials do not close that map.

Replication risk is material. Foundation models and compute are purchasable, while competitors can combine similar tools with their own workflow data. The defensible layer, if any, is the feedback loop created by installed workflow, trusted permissions, domain ontology and distribution. Switching costs can preserve adoption, although they can also slow deployment and leave value with model/cloud suppliers. Vendor concentration, inference cost and model drift are therefore part of capture economics.

## Evidence ledger and negative evidence

|---|---|---|
| AI-capable product or internal workflow exists | Product pages support availability; E1/E2 | Availability is not active usage |
| Proprietary/permissioned data improves relevance | Mechanistically plausible | Rights, coverage and ablation are undisclosed |
| AI raises revenue or lowers cost | E0/E1 hypothesis | Pricing, mix, macro demand and ordinary automation confound |
| Incumbent captures the surplus | Unproven | Cloud/model vendors, customers and competitors may capture it |

The strongest negative evidence is the missing causal bridge. The sources do not disclose an eligible-workload denominator, treatment/control design, before/after quality distribution, full implementation and inference cost, or an auditable bridge to segment profit. Management examples may be selected successes. A faster task can create more review work, errors or consumption rather than cash savings. Where safety, regulation or customer trust matter, human review limits labor removal. No public evidence reviewed establishes that the cited AI surface caused a material share of FY2025 profit.

## Measurement design and decision use

A useful next disclosure would separate technical performance from economic realization. The technical layer should report the frozen model version, evaluation set, error taxonomy and drift; the operating layer should report eligible cases, attempted cases, accepted recommendations, overrides and downstream quality; the finance layer should show gross benefit, implementation expense, recurring inference/vendor cost, displaced spend and timing. All three layers need the same cohort and period. Otherwise a high acceptance rate can coexist with no cash benefit, and a favorable cost trend can be driven by unrelated restructuring.

Selection is another risk. Early adopters, easier cases and high-performing sites often enter first. A credible design would randomize where feasible or use matched sites/workflows with pre-period trends, fixed inclusion rules and confidence intervals. It would retain failed runs and human corrections rather than measure only completed outputs. Quality guardrails should include customer harm, regulatory exceptions, rework and tail latency. The company should disclose whether automation removes a step, shifts it to another team, or merely increases capacity.

The committee can use a staged evidence rule. Product availability supports E1. Authenticated production use with an eligible-workload denominator supports E2. A persistent matched operational lift supports E3. A finance reconciliation net of all costs is needed for E4. Until those steps are met, the arithmetic above defines what evidence must explain; it does not convert management claims into an earnings contribution. This design also makes the thesis falsifiable within a defined reporting cycle rather than waiting for broad margin movement that has many causes.

## Falsifiers and gates

The thesis fails or weakens if: (1) matched cohorts show no durable improvement after quality adjustment; (2) adoption stalls outside pilots; (3) errors, review and compute consume gross benefit; (4) rights prevent learning across the useful population; or (5) competitors reproduce outcomes and price away gains. Evidence would strengthen with audited cohort definitions, usage denominators, unit-cost curves, renewal/attach cohorts and a finance reconciliation.

Confidence is **medium** on mechanism, **low-to-medium** on deployment breadth and **low** on incremental capture. G0 is met through identifiable sources and arithmetic. G1 is provisionally met subject to conventional financial review. G2 is not passed because causal operating evidence and rights are incomplete. G3 valuation and G4 portfolio construction are explicitly deferred per the user’s instruction.
