## Provisional conclusion

Nike has a durable conventional franchise based on brand, athlete relationships, product archive, membership demand signals and global distribution. The public evidence supports specific AI capabilities and, in some cases, management-reported operational use. It does not provide a controlled, quality-adjusted company result tied to retained incremental cash. The central underwriting issue is capture: operational improvement may pass to customers, be absorbed by implementation, already sit in the reported baseline, or be dominated by cycle and mix. The dossier therefore keeps G2 provisional and treats E3/E4 as unestablished.

## Conventional business and financial anchor

Nike reports Nike Brand by geography, Converse and corporate; wholesale and direct channels have different economics. Customer value arises from dependable execution, integration and domain expertise, not data volume alone. Competitive advantage must survive Adidas and fast-moving direct brands and marketplaces with their own demand data. Switching costs and distribution help, but contracts, interoperability and procurement can reallocate gains.

For FY2025 ended 31 May 2025, the opened company reporting identifies **about $46.3bn revenue**, **about $4.3bn earnings before interest and taxes scale**, and **operating cash flow roughly $3.7bn and capital expenditure below $1bn**. The capital context is that cash and investments provide resilience, though leases and inventory commitments matter. Reported and adjusted measures are not interchangeable: the materiality bridge below uses the explicitly labelled profit measure or proxy and does not present it as normalized earnings. Acquisitions, currency, product/geographic mix, restructuring, commodity or equipment cycles, and accounting changes can confound comparisons. The latest annual-report/results hub should be reopened for exact statement lines during lead review.

The [FY2026 issuer release](https://about.nike.com/en/newsroom/releases/nike-inc-reports-fiscal-2026-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-results) reports full-year revenue of $46.4bn, flat reported and down 2% currency-neutral. Nike A.I.R. is a named generative-design exploration with athletes, supporting E1 creative capability, not demand-forecast deployment or commercial efficacy. The core adverse evidence is continued channel/China weakness and stronger performance brands.

The ordinary baseline already contains deployed analytics, automation and machine learning. Capital allocation must fund maintenance, growth investment, obligations and shareholder distributions before any incremental AI benefit. Financial scale permits experimentation, but it does not demonstrate quality, adoption or capture.

## Measurement contract and retained economics

Pre-register **full-price sell-through, inventory turns, forecast error by SKU/market, return rate and contribution after marketing**. Compare randomized or carefully matched sites, clients, products or cohorts against the current production system, including existing ML. Define the eligible workload before rollout; hold service, safety, accuracy, risk and customer-mix quality constant. Report sample size, duration, confidence interval, adoption, exception/rework rate and all human/model/implementation cost. The known public result is insufficient for E3. A future annual report, product cohort study or customer renewal disclosure linking the KPI to contribution could close the gap.

The framework's five-year research convention is **$190m** for this packet. 5%×$4.3bn profit-scale proxy=$190m. A $3bn eligible SG&A/operations pool×10%×50%×50%−$60m=$15m. Avoided markdown requires matched seasons and cannot be inferred from total gross margin. Assumption inputs are illustrative and reproducible. They are neither guidance nor valuation. Released time counts only if removed or redeployed to valued output; the same capacity cannot be counted as both cost saving and revenue. Capital, working capital and tax would need separate treatment.

## Competing hypotheses, falsifiers and gates

**Strongest positive hypothesis:** proprietary workflow context and distribution let Nike deploy validated AI faster than standalone vendors, with better feedback and a retained service, pricing, throughput or capital benefit. **Strongest negative hypothesis:** model capability commoditizes, customer rights prevent pooling, implementation and verification absorb gains, and Adidas or fast-moving direct brands and marketplaces with their own demand data forces pass-through.

Three falsifiers are: (1) matched deployments fail to improve full-price sell-through, inventory turns, forecast error by SKU/market, return rate and contribution after marketing at equal quality; (2) five-year retained benefit after recurring AI and implementation cost remains below the stated 5% hurdle; (3) contracts, portability or competitor performance show that the alleged data/workflow advantage is readily replicated. Unresolved questions are eligible-workload share, exact production penetration, customer-level retention/pricing, recurring model cost, contract training rights and the latest post-FY2026 adverse development.

G0 passes because asset, mechanism, capture route and falsifiers are explicit. G1 is provisionally supported by the conventional franchise and financial capacity, subject to latest-period refresh and sector risks. G2 remains **open**: scoped E1/E2 can support further testing, but E3/E4 are absent. G3 and G4 are **deferred by user**. Confidence is **medium** for mechanism, **low-to-medium** for deployment breadth and **low** for capture.
