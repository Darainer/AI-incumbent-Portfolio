# Cybersecurity and identity software

**Area:** I08 · **Sector coverage:** Information Technology · **Version:** 0.1 · **As of:** 2026-09-11

**Status: research hypothesis.** Mechanisms and tests below are analyst proposals, not established results for all companies in the area. The evidence paragraph identifies what the cited sources actually support. Company-level verification and valuation remain separate gates.

## The incumbent asset

Threat telemetry, endpoint distribution, response workflows and security expertise. Research must establish who owns each input, what can be licensed or replicated, and whether usage rights permit inference, retrieval, product improvement or pooled training. Those are four different permissions.

## The AI advantage

AI can triage incidents, correlate signals and accelerate response while attackers also use AI.

The proposed feedback loop is operational: additional activity creates observed outcomes, which improve the next decision only if the outcomes are accessible, representative and lawfully reusable. A faster interface alone does not establish a learning advantage. Separate established optimization or machine learning from incremental generative or agentic capabilities.

## Who retains the gain

Trusted security platforms can retain spending if detection quality and integration outperform cheaper bundled tools. Identify the invoice, contract, patent, fee schedule or internal cost line through which value reaches shareholders. Compare the benefit with spending on data preparation, inference, integration, validation and physical deployment.

## Research criteria

| Test | Evidence required | Failure or unresolved outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Asset advantage | Inventory of the specific inputs above, provenance, data contracts and a credible replication-cost comparison | Mere data volume, public records, or access without usage rights |
| Incremental AI lift | Use independent adversarial evaluations and production incident cohorts. Never substitute demo detection for real-world coverage. | A demo, a self-reported time estimate or a comparison against an obsolete baseline |
| Operational deployment | Production scope, eligible workload denominator, named process owner and exception rate | Pilot activity with no scaled workflow adoption |
| Economic capture | Reconcile the KPIs below to segment cash earnings and customer pricing | Gains absorbed by price cuts, revenue cannibalization or supplier fees |
| Durability | Compare rival offerings, customer portability and the rate of new useful feedback | A generic model or common vendor reproduces the benefit |
| Quality and risk | Missed incidents, adversarial evasion and correlated outage risk | Material harm, hidden liabilities or quality decline |

## Measurement contract

| KPI | Baseline and comparator | Promotion criterion | Controls |
|---|---|---|---|
| False positives at fixed detection rate | Obtain a pre-deployment baseline and matched comparison | Require sustained improvement net of full implementation cost | Missed incidents, adversarial evasion and correlated outage risk |
| analyst hours per incident | Obtain a pre-deployment baseline and matched comparison | Require sustained improvement net of full implementation cost | Missed incidents, adversarial evasion and correlated outage risk |
| net retention | Obtain a pre-deployment baseline and matched comparison | Require sustained improvement net of full implementation cost | Missed incidents, adversarial evasion and correlated outage risk |

Set a company-specific minimum economically meaningful effect before examining results. Quantify sample size, uncertainty and seasonal or cohort effects. Default financial materiality trigger: a credible path to at least 5% of normalized operating profit within five years. This is an analyst screening convention, not an observed industry threshold. Use risk-adjusted pipeline economics for pharma and capital-adjusted earnings for financial firms.

**Expected observation window:** 6–24 months. This is a research planning assumption, not a forecast.

## Falsification

Automation adds alert volume, misses novel attacks or cannot offset platform bundling. Downgrade the thesis if this persists in a properly controlled evaluation. Lack of disclosed evidence means unproven, not necessarily disproven.
