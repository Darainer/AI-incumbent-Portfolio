# Property ownership, REITs and data centers

**Area:** R01 · **Sector coverage:** Real Estate · **Version:** 0.1 · **As of:** 2026-09-11

**Status: research hypothesis.** Mechanisms and tests below are analyst proposals, not established results for all companies in the area. The evidence paragraph identifies what the cited sources actually support. Company-level verification and valuation remain separate gates.

## The incumbent asset

Locations, tenant relationships, building systems and operating scale. Research must establish who owns each input, what can be licensed or replicated, and whether usage rights permit inference, retrieval, product improvement or pooled training. Those are four different permissions.

## The AI advantage

AI can improve maintenance, leasing support and energy management. Data-center rent growth is an AI-demand thesis with different economics.

The proposed feedback loop is operational: additional activity creates observed outcomes, which improve the next decision only if the outcomes are accessible, representative and lawfully reusable. A faster interface alone does not establish a learning advantage. Separate established optimization or machine learning from incremental generative or agentic capabilities.

## Who retains the gain

Lease structure determines who keeps operating savings. Strong locations can support occupancy but do not guarantee an AI moat. Identify the invoice, contract, patent, fee schedule or internal cost line through which value reaches shareholders. Compare the benefit with spending on data preparation, inference, integration, validation and physical deployment.

## Research criteria

| Test | Evidence required | Failure or unresolved outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Asset advantage | Inventory of the specific inputs above, provenance, data contracts and a credible replication-cost comparison | Mere data volume, public records, or access without usage rights |
| Incremental AI lift | Compare matched properties under identical lease structures. Separate rental market changes from technology effects. | A demo, a self-reported time estimate or a comparison against an obsolete baseline |
| Operational deployment | Production scope, eligible workload denominator, named process owner and exception rate | Pilot activity with no scaled workflow adoption |
| Economic capture | Reconcile the KPIs below to segment cash earnings and customer pricing | Gains absorbed by price cuts, revenue cannibalization or supplier fees |
| Durability | Compare rival offerings, customer portability and the rate of new useful feedback | A generic model or common vendor reproduces the benefit |
| Quality and risk | Tenant satisfaction, tenant concentration, capex and financing costs | Material harm, hidden liabilities or quality decline |

## Measurement contract

| KPI | Baseline and comparator | Promotion criterion | Controls |
|---|---|---|---|
| Same-property NOI at fixed occupancy | Obtain a pre-deployment baseline and matched comparison | Require sustained improvement net of full implementation cost | Tenant satisfaction, tenant concentration, capex and financing costs |
| owner-retained energy savings | Obtain a pre-deployment baseline and matched comparison | Require sustained improvement net of full implementation cost | Tenant satisfaction, tenant concentration, capex and financing costs |
| leasing cost | Obtain a pre-deployment baseline and matched comparison | Require sustained improvement net of full implementation cost | Tenant satisfaction, tenant concentration, capex and financing costs |

Set a company-specific minimum economically meaningful effect before examining results. Quantify sample size, uncertainty and seasonal or cohort effects. Default financial materiality trigger: a credible path to at least 5% of normalized operating profit within five years. This is an analyst screening convention, not an observed industry threshold. Use risk-adjusted pipeline economics for pharma and capital-adjusted earnings for financial firms.

**Expected observation window:** 1–3 years. This is a research planning assumption, not a forecast.

## Falsification

Tenants receive all savings or debt and obsolescence overwhelm small operating benefits. Downgrade the thesis if this persists in a properly controlled evaluation. Lack of disclosed evidence means unproven, not necessarily disproven.
