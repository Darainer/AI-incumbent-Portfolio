# Credit data, exchanges and financial intelligence

**Area:** F05 · **Sector coverage:** Financials · **Version:** 0.1 · **As of:** 2026-09-11

**Status: research hypothesis.** Mechanisms and tests below are analyst proposals, not established results for all companies in the area. The evidence paragraph identifies what the cited sources actually support. Company-level verification and valuation remain separate gates.

## The incumbent asset

Permissioned credit histories, market records, ratings expertise and licensed data feeds. Research must establish who owns each input, what can be licensed or replicated, and whether usage rights permit inference, retrieval, product improvement or pooled training. Those are four different permissions.

## The AI advantage

AI can turn curated records into underwriting, research and compliance decisions with less manual reconciliation.

The proposed feedback loop is operational: additional activity creates observed outcomes, which improve the next decision only if the outcomes are accessible, representative and lawfully reusable. A faster interface alone does not establish a learning advantage. Separate established optimization or machine learning from incremental generative or agentic capabilities.

## Who retains the gain

Licenses, data entitlements and embedded decision workflows can support paid tools. Public facts alone are a weak moat. Identify the invoice, contract, patent, fee schedule or internal cost line through which value reaches shareholders. Compare the benefit with spending on data preparation, inference, integration, validation and physical deployment.

## Research criteria

| Test | Evidence required | Failure or unresolved outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Asset advantage | Inventory of the specific inputs above, provenance, data contracts and a credible replication-cost comparison | Mere data volume, public records, or access without usage rights |
| Incremental AI lift | Compare incumbent and challenger performance on identical lawful datasets. Then add proprietary features to isolate their marginal value. | A demo, a self-reported time estimate or a comparison against an obsolete baseline |
| Operational deployment | Production scope, eligible workload denominator, named process owner and exception rate | Pilot activity with no scaled workflow adoption |
| Economic capture | Reconcile the KPIs below to segment cash earnings and customer pricing | Gains absorbed by price cuts, revenue cannibalization or supplier fees |
| Durability | Compare rival offerings, customer portability and the rate of new useful feedback | A generic model or common vendor reproduces the benefit |
| Quality and risk | Data provenance, model leakage, licensing costs and customer concentration | Material harm, hidden liabilities or quality decline |

## Measurement contract

| KPI | Baseline and comparator | Promotion criterion | Controls |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paid AI attach | Obtain a pre-deployment baseline and matched comparison | Require sustained improvement net of full implementation cost | Data provenance, model leakage, licensing costs and customer concentration |
| net retention | Obtain a pre-deployment baseline and matched comparison | Require sustained improvement net of full implementation cost | Data provenance, model leakage, licensing costs and customer concentration |
| analyst or onboarding time at fixed error rate | Obtain a pre-deployment baseline and matched comparison | Require sustained improvement net of full implementation cost | Data provenance, model leakage, licensing costs and customer concentration |

Set a company-specific minimum economically meaningful effect before examining results. Quantify sample size, uncertainty and seasonal or cohort effects. Default financial materiality trigger: a credible path to at least 5% of normalized operating profit within five years. This is an analyst screening convention, not an observed industry threshold. Use risk-adjusted pipeline economics for pharma and capital-adjusted earnings for financial firms.

**Expected observation window:** 1–3 years. This is a research planning assumption, not a forecast.

## Falsification

Customers recreate the product with alternative data, or suppliers capture the AI revenue. Downgrade the thesis if this persists in a properly controlled evaluation. Lack of disclosed evidence means unproven, not necessarily disproven.
