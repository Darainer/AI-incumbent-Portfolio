## Decision statement

State what the business owns, exactly how AI could improve economics, what shareholders retain and what the current valuation requires. Label facts, inferences and assumptions. Separate confidence in the mechanism, deployment, economic capture and valuation.

## Existing business and financial resilience

Document segments, customers, recurring economics, competitive advantage, cyclicality, financial obligations and management's capital allocation. Explain why the business remains viable if incremental AI upside is zero. Assess governance and jurisdiction-specific exposure.

## Falsification and decision

State three company-specific conditions that would reduce conviction. Separate an AI thesis failure from a core-business failure and a price becoming unattractive. Review portfolio overlap by underlying driver, supplier and geography. Document the decision and unresolved gates. An incomplete gate cannot become a pass through a high score.
