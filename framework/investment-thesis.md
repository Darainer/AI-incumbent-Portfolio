# The value of incumbent assets in an AI economy

## The proposition

As capable AI becomes more widely available, the ability to apply it to valuable real-world activity may become more important. Established companies already possess customer access, trusted workflows, scientific expertise, physical infrastructure and knowledge that is difficult to recreate. AI can increase the output of those assets.

Our investment hypothesis is that some incumbents can turn that productivity into durable cash earnings without needing to own the leading foundation model. The opportunity is conditional: the company must have usable rights, effective deployment, a mechanism to retain the gain and a valuation that leaves room for the investor.

## Four routes to economic value

| Route | Incumbent advantage | Economic result to test |
|---|---|---|
| Better decisions | Risk, transaction and customer context | Lower losses or more profitable activity at the same risk |
| More useful scientific output | Experiments, clinical knowledge and validation capacity | Better stage-adjusted R&D output after full development cost |
| More work inside existing workflows | Content, permissions, systems of record and distribution | Higher retained contribution despite seat substitution and model costs |
| More output from physical assets | Installed equipment, process knowledge and service networks | Better utilization, quality and cash return on invested capital |

These routes can coexist in several firms. Expanding demand can support multiple winners, particularly when better products enable previously uneconomic activity. An oligopoly is a possible outcome, not a guarantee. Greater supply can also push prices down and transfer value to customers.

## Where we begin

Professional information and vertical workflows offer relatively short feedback cycles and a visible monetization question. Payments and insurance place high value on better marginal decisions. Industrial systems connect AI to scarce physical assets. Pharma offers potentially large benefits with slower, harder clinical proof.

This order reflects research tractability, not a claim that one sector offers the best current returns. The first 18-company queue includes several mechanisms and negative cases. We will require a stronger evidential burden where outcomes are delayed or the cost of error is high.

## What is different from a generic AI portfolio

The unit of analysis is the incumbent's economic engine. Suppliers of AI infrastructure remain relevant, but increased demand for compute is distinct from improved productivity within an existing business. Conglomerates require segment analysis so those exposures are not conflated.

The key scarce asset may be trusted content, an installed base or execution rights rather than training data. A workflow can become more valuable even when customer records remain isolated. Conversely, possessing records without usable labels, permission or operational integration can confer little advantage.

## The investor's test

The market may overlook productivity embedded inside conventional sector labels. It may also already price that productivity generously. We will value the conventional business, estimate incremental AI effects without double counting, and reverse the share price into required assumptions.

The research must answer: what does the company own, what changes operationally, who gets paid, how long does the advantage last, and how much is already priced in?

## Confidence and failure conditions

Confidence is high that AI can improve some well-specified tasks, based on observed research and deployed use cases. Confidence is moderate in the broader incumbent mechanism and low in a universal advantage or any unresearched security's mispricing. Company-specific confidence must be earned.

The regime fails where assets are unusable, entrants can replicate the benefit, organizations cannot deploy, physical validation remains the bottleneck, or customers and suppliers capture all the surplus. It also fails as an investment if the price already discounts more improvement than the company can deliver.

The ambition is to identify a durable economic shift early. The research standard is to be precise enough that the thesis can be wrong.
