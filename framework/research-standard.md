# Research standard

Version 0.1 · 11 September 2026

## Investment proposition

AI can increase the economic output of scarce incumbent assets. The investable opportunity exists only where a company can deploy the technology, preserve quality, retain part of the benefit and offer an adequate return at the price paid. None of those steps follows automatically from company size or data volume.

The strongest candidate owns a durable business before incremental AI upside. The research process must also discover incumbents whose economics deteriorate as AI reduces switching costs, changes distribution or commoditizes labor.

## The causal chain

1. Identify the scarce asset and its lawful usable scope.
2. Specify the decision or task AI improves relative to the current best alternative.
3. Demonstrate deployment into a material share of real activity.
4. Measure output and quality after human review, integration and physical constraints.
5. Determine how savings, new revenue or reduced losses divide among suppliers, workers, customers and shareholders.
6. Establish whether better activity produces additional useful feedback and durable advantage.
7. Compare the resulting cash flows with what the current valuation already requires.

Every link requires evidence. Scale is an input, not a conclusion. A company can have a compelling product while its stock already prices in more success than is plausible.

## Evidence ladder

| Stage | What has been established | What has not |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | An analyst has formulated an asset and mechanism hypothesis | No direct company-specific evidence reviewed |
| E1 | A primary source identifies a product, capability, programme or intended use | Material production deployment and economic impact |
| E2 | A company or identified customer reports actual production use | Causal attribution or net shareholder benefit |
| E3 | A controlled or carefully matched comparison shows improvement at equal quality | Durable economic capture through competition and repricing |
| E4 | Incremental benefit reconciles to cash economics and persists across relevant renewals or cohorts | A guarantee of future advantage or an attractive current valuation |

Evidence stage describes the strongest documented claim, not the entire corporation. No company is E3 merely because a study in the same industry found a positive result. A deployment claim may be management-reported. Record that explicitly. Unknown does not mean absent, and absence of disclosed evidence does not prove failure.

## Facts, inferences and assumptions

**Fact:** cite a precise source, publication date, access date, page or section and scope. Favor filings, product documentation, regulatory publications and original research. A marketing page establishes what its author offers or claims, not independent effectiveness.

**Inference:** explain which observations support it and what competing explanation remains. For example, higher renewal may reflect bundled pricing or migration rather than AI quality.

**Assumption:** declare a model input before evaluating outcomes. Use sensitivity analysis, not invented precision. Do not silently fill unavailable values with peer averages.

For material underwriting decisions, obtain a filing or results source, a product/deployment source and a challenger or customer source. A second source repeating the first press release is not independent corroboration.

## Data advantage audit

For every alleged data moat, document data origin, ownership, exclusivity, permitted retrieval, permitted inference, permitted training, permitted cross-customer learning, consent, retention and portability. Identify labels, counterfactual coverage, drift and the cost of cleaning historical records. Repeated transactions are useful feedback only if their outcomes are observable and add predictive information.

Distinguish three advantages: proprietary content, customer-specific context and pooled learning. A company may have the first two without the third. Thomson Reuters' AI FAQ is a concrete reason to test these separately [S05](../sources/README.md).

## Measurement and attribution

Pre-register the relevant KPI, quality constraint and minimum economically meaningful effect. Use randomized rollout where feasible, otherwise matched cohorts or a defensible difference-in-differences design. Record sample size, duration, confidence intervals and limitations. Evaluate against the existing production system, including classical ML and optimization, rather than a deliberately weak manual baseline.

Include deployment failures and abandoned trials. Control for price, mix, acquisitions, staffing, commodity cycles, credit conditions and capital upgrades. Measure end-to-end completion rather than tokens, logins, drafts or suggested actions. Time freed is not cash saved until capacity is removed or redeployed into valued output.

Customer support research found a 15% average increase in issues resolved per hour in its studied setting [S01](../sources/README.md). METR's early-2025 developer trial found a slowdown in a different setting, and its 2026 update warns about measurement and selection challenges [S02, S18](../sources/README.md). These studies support task-specific measurement, not a universal productivity percentage.

## Promotion gates

| Gate | Required result |
|---|---|
| G0: candidate | Explicit asset, mechanism, capture hypothesis, falsifier and industry mapping |
| G1: qualified business | Verify core business durability, financial resilience, governance and lawful usable assets |
| G2: operating thesis | At least E2 plus a credible route to controlled evidence and material net economic impact |
| G3: valued opportunity | Dated price and share count, conventional-business downside, explicit AI scenarios and market-implied expectations |
| G4: portfolio eligible | Risk, liquidity, concentration and correlation reviewed, with a written sizing and exit rationale |

A suggested research materiality hurdle is a credible path to at least 5% of normalized operating profit in five years. It is an analyst convention, not an empirical law. Use adjusted risk and capital measures for financial firms and risk-adjusted clinical economics for pharma. Small firms and radically different economics may justify an explicitly documented alternative.

No score overrides a failed gate. Every company in the initial universe is research-only. No automatic buy labels, price alerts, trade instructions or portfolio weights follow from this release.

## Review discipline

Refresh source dates and material developments at each investment decision. Update operational evidence at relevant reporting events, clinical cohorts or contract renewals. Assign an owner and next review date only when research work is actually scheduled. This repository does not create automated monitoring tasks.
